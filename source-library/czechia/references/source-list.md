# Czechia — national legal sources for free movement and family members

**Inventory date:** 30 October 2026. **Status:** 67 source-register records, including 67 scoped official-reference locator decisions. Forty-five dated local source-text copies are accepted within their recorded limits (21 e-Sbírka legal texts, 13 guidance pages, 6 blank forms with native PDFs, and 5 limited discovery/index snapshots); two incomplete captures have locator notes only. Decree 429/2010's 2020-02-01 text is fully compared with the official dated e-Sbírka version; applicability to EU-family routes remains pending targeted human review. Three additional current official legal texts—Acts 435/2004, 48/1997 and 117/1995—were captured directly from e-Sbírka on 30 October 2026 after the matching Legalize-CZ candidates failed full-body checks. Official URLs are retained as provenance/version locators; a link alone is not a source-text document. The source documents are the retained local text files under `documents/`. The MFA local-consular page is accepted only for long-term visa and long-term/permanent residence guidance. No topic is accepted and country review remains incomplete. All 43 former candidate files have a disposition; no files remain under `candidates/`. The source acceptance register records local-copy hashes and artifact-specific limits. See the [source acceptance register](source-acceptance.json). **Scope:** Czech national legislation, official authorities, courts, and oversight materials relevant to EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, residence, EU-family visa processing, civil-status evidence, procedure, and remedies. EU legislation and CJEU judgments are excluded because they belong in the EU core. Primary legal texts are in Czech; official English guidance is marked separately.
