The optional e-Sbírka adapter uses the public, unauthenticated page-data service. It resolves each stable act record to the publisher's current effective version, captures ordered text-fragment pages, and converts XHTML to Markdown; it does not use the separately documented registration-gated REST API. The 20 e-Sbírka current-version source copies (17 retrieved 19 October and 3 captured 30 October 2026) and the separately verified Decree 429/2010 copy are accepted only within the version and limits recorded in the acceptance register. Recheck the stable record for later use. Preserve the publisher's “informative” label and displayed effective date; consult a particular promulgation only when a known discrepancy or commencement issue makes it material.

Both local adapters write raw responses, temporary conversions, and run state under the standard project `temp/source-library/czechia/work/` tree. The 62 raw response files from 19 October 2026 remain there and are indexed in the Monitor local research archive. The additional eight exact official metadata/page responses for Acts 435/2004, 48/1997 and 117/1995 are archived with per-response hashes at `captures/czechia-official-source-capture-2026-10-30/Czechia/czechia/esbirka/retrieval-manifest.json`. The 17 October e-Sbírka conversions were replayed from their saved responses on 25 October 2026; the three new 30 October copies retain complete ordered-fragment counts, raw-response hashes and converted-document hashes. Their retrieval manifests are indexed in Monitor under `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/e-sbirka/retrieval-manifest.json` and `captures/czechia-candidate-promotion-2026-10-25/Czechia/czechia/html-guidance/retrieval-manifest.json`. Changed conversions stay in project temp until reviewed and archived through Monitor; the pack has no `candidates/` directory.

The separate 13-record Monitor capture batch was dispositioned individually: six native form PDFs and five dated page captures were retained under `documents/`; two incomplete NSS and UOOU captures became official-link locator notes. The original raw HTML bodies for the seven page captures are not retained, so those historical HTML conversions cannot be replayed from their original response bytes. Four individual Czech court decisions now have maintained case summaries; they remain examples, not a repeatable court search. Other link-only references have no local copies. Coverage, dependencies, continuing discovery, and country review remain partial.

The retrieval register routes 13 identified Czech-language MOI and gov.cz guidance pages through the official HTML adapter, and four individual court decisions through a case-number-validated HTML/PDF converter. `scripts/sync-czechia.mjs --sync` stages outputs as review candidates under the project temp tree; `--verify` and `--reconvert` replay saved bytes, and `--ack <source-id>` copies a manually reviewed guidance or decision candidate under `documents/`. Decree 429/2010 now has an accepted local copy for the version effective 2020-02-01. Its candidate body was fully compared with the rendered official e-Sbírka version and its omitted header and preamble were supplied from e-Sbírka; the candidate-specific conversion record is in `references/legalize-cz-conversion-rules.md`. The remaining review concerns only applicability to EU-family routes. Any later update must use the official dated version and repeat the full comparison. These routes are implemented as acquisition methods, not evidence of retrieval, acceptance, or topic coverage.

The inventory covers Czech national law and official administrative, consular, court, and oversight materials relevant to EU free movement and family members. It is not an exhaustive account of Czech law. Official Czech guidance also describes similar free-movement rights for Icelandic, Liechtenstein, Norwegian, and Swiss citizens; Switzerland is not an EU or EEA EFTA member, and UK Withdrawal Agreement cases remain separate.
