Submit a complaint

Once we receive your complaint (suggestion or notification), we will get back to you as soon as possible. We will contact you within 30 days at the latest.

On-line

Fill in an interactive on-line form on our website portal.ochrance.cz

Submit on-line

By e-mail

Download the form, fill it in and send it to podatelna@ochrance.cz

  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman – to fill in on the computer
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman about discrimination – to fill in on the computer

By mail

Download the form, print it and send it filled out to the following address:

Office of the Public Defender of Rights and Defender of Children's Rights
Údolní 39
602 00 Brno

  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman – to print and fill in by hand
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman – to fill in on the computer
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman about discrimination – to print and fill in by hand
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman about discrimination – to fill in on the computer

In person (only in our office in Brno)

You can also download the form and bring it to us already filled out, or bring a blank copy – we will help you to complete and submit it. Visit us on monday or wednesday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. at:

Office of the Public Defender of Rights and Defender of Children's Rights
Údolní 39
602 00 Brno

  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman – to print and fill in by hand
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman – to fill in on the computer
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman about discrimination – to print and fill in by hand
  • Submission (complaint) to the Ombudsman about discrimination – to fill in on the computer
