# Belgium source review and sustainability assessment

Review date: 24 October 2026

## Decision

The register contains 48 source records. Forty-seven official links are accepted within their recorded scope; the superseded IBZ `/family` route is not relied upon. Thirty-one local copies are accepted: nine federal Justel legal texts; fifteen official guidance, procedure and oversight snapshots; three IBZ form/specimen PDFs; the FPS Foreign Affairs visa-routing matrix; current Walloon and Flemish regional public-information laws; and the January 2025 French RvV/CCE selected-jurisprudence issue. The Federal Justice adoption guidance, the short IBZ family-reunification landing page, and the French DPA complaint page are accepted as date-bounded administrative snapshots. No legal topic is accepted as adequately covered, and Belgium review is not complete. Of eleven historical converted review artifacts, three are now final documents and eight are closed with link-only or superseded dispositions; none remains unresolved.

For ordinary use, prefer a clearly identified current government consolidation. When the exact edition or scope is unclear, consult the official source directly; the whole official site/service is listed in `source-acceptance.json` for human review. The review does not require Gazette-fragment reconstruction, API registration, a custom capture/conversion rule, local hashes, replay, or a scheduled updater.

- The French APD complaint page was directly retrieved on 2026-10-24 with the full body and a raw hash matching the earlier capture; a web-cached view still showed maintenance text. The page is accepted as a date-bounded snapshot. Its linked paper form is separate and remains unaccepted pending version review.
- Annex 19ter is accepted only as the specimen linked from the current IBZ index. The legacy filename does not establish its current revision or classification.
- The native 12 October 2026 Foreign Affairs competence-matrix PDF is accepted as a dated routing aid after checking its structure and sample UK/UAE rows. The UK and UAE mission pages are examples, not a review of every mission.
- The visa GDPR page dated 24 February 2026 is the preferred current operational privacy page. The separate 2022 privacy statement is historical.
- Annexes 19 and 20 are accepted as exact native form PDFs linked from the current IBZ index; their publisher filenames identify 2025 specimens. Annex 19ter is preserved as the two-page specimen linked from the current IBZ index; its SPECIMEN watermark means it is not a blank operational form. The January 2025 RvV/CCE monthly issue is preserved under `documents/case_law/` as a selective historical publication. - The 2018 Data Protection Act is now linked through official French and Dutch Justel locators. Federal government pages identify the statute and NUMAC; Justel is offered for direct review because its live record did not render in this browser check.

It expressly states that the Official Gazette original is legally binding. The exact current records for the 1995 access-to-documents decree and the 2022 information/complaints decree still need to be found in that database.
- The French Community’s 1994 publicity decree, COCOF operational access procedure, and the 2019 Brussels joint access decree are now linked. Keep their institutional scopes separate. The 1997 provinces/municipalities law is linked as a statute locator; its current regional/local application must be checked before use.
