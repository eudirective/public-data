## Captured official text

Family members of a citizen from the EU or from one of the Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein also have the right to move and reside freely on the territory of EU Member states. However, this right is subject to conditions.

More info on [the right of entry and residence of family members who are themselves EU citizens](https://dofi.ibz.be/en/themes/citizens-european-union/family-reunification/family-reunification-between-eu-citizens). (Work in progress)
