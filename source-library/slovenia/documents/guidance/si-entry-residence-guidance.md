## Verified page scope

The Slovenian-language parent page separates EU citizens and family members, Swiss nationals and family members, third-country nationals, visa regimes and applications, foreigner address registration, and legislation. It links to the underlying GOV.SI service records, consular information and the Foreigners Act.

The page states, among other things, that:

- EU citizens may enter with a valid identity card or passport and may stay for the first three months without residence registration; a person planning a longer stay applies for a residence-registration certificate at an administrative unit. The page says the relevant Foreigners Act provisions also apply to EEA citizens and their family members.
- A non-EU family member of an EU or Slovenian citizen may stay for 90 days after entry without a residence permit; an application is required before that period ends for a longer stay.
- It treats Swiss citizens and their family members separately.
- Third-country entry and residence rules depend on purpose, duration and visa requirement. It describes C and D visas, links application forms and mission channels, and directs users to country-specific supporting-document lists.
- It describes local registration duties for holders of residence permits or visas and links to relevant e-services.

These are summaries of the page as displayed, not independent statements of current law. The parent page uses specified categories and contains time-sensitive procedure and visa information; use the linked service page, current law and competent authority for a particular route. Do not infer that EU-family rules, Slovenian-citizen sponsor rules and Swiss/AFMP routes are identical.
