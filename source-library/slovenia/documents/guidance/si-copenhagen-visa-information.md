## Verified page scope

The Slovenian-language GOV.SI page is a mission-specific information and navigation page. It states that citizens of Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden do not need a visa to enter Slovenia. Third-country nationals legally residing in the countries accredited to the embassy are directed to the European Commission visa information to check visa obligation. The page sends readers to the central GOV.SI entry/residence topic for visa and residence-permit details.

The page also provides mission contacts, consular information and appointment direction. It says applications and visits are accepted by prior appointment and lists a 2026 closure calendar. These are post-specific, time-sensitive operational details and must be checked on the live page before use.

## Limits and maintenance

This record captures one Copenhagen mission page. It does not establish other posts' accreditation, representation arrangements, appointment systems, checklists, fees or filing rules, and it does not provide a complete Slovenian visa application checklist. For an applicant, confirm their lawful residence, competent mission and any Schengen representation arrangement on the current official page.

Recheck the displayed page date, visa links, appointment instructions and closure notices at the point of use. Keep visa requirements distinct from residence authorization and from EU/EEA or Swiss family-member residence routes.
