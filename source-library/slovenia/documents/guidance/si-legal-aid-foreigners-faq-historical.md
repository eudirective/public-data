## What the page says

The Slovenian FAQ answer states that, under the same conditions as Slovenian citizens, free legal aid is available to:

- persons residing in Slovenia at the time of application on the basis of a temporary or permanent Slovenian residence permit;
- stateless persons lawfully residing in Slovenia; and
- persons lawfully residing in another EU Member State.

It says other foreign nationals may qualify under reciprocity or where an international treaty provides for it.

This is a concise official FAQ answer, not a complete explanation of the Free Legal Aid Act (ZBPP), financial eligibility, merits screening, procedural requirements, exceptions, or the competent authority's assessment.
