## Scope

The General Administrative Procedure Act (Zakon o splošnem upravnem postopku, ZUP) supplies the general procedure for state and other public bodies, local self-government bodies, and holders of public authority when they decide administrative matters about a person's or entity's rights, obligations or legal interests.

The Act defines administrative matters and sets the general procedural framework. It also provides for subsidiary use in other public-law matters where a special procedure does not govern. Where a field-specific law establishes a different or special administrative procedure, that law governs the issue it regulates and ZUP fills procedural questions it leaves open. Do not assume every rule in ZUP applies unchanged to an immigration proceeding or other special statutory process.

The statute covers foundational principles and authority, parties and representation, language, service, evidence and participation, decisions, administrative appeals, execution and extraordinary remedies. This note is an orientation to the official reference and its version, not an article-by-article account or an individual remedy assessment.

## Current PISRS version and amendment history

The official PISRS record identifies the Act as **Zakon o splošnem upravnem postopku (ZUP)**, ID **ZAKO1603**, with source identifier **SOP 1999-01-3777**. On review, PISRS search/index metadata identified **unofficial consolidated text NPB 14**, listed amendments through **ZUP-I, Official Gazette RS 85/2025**, and gave **7 February 2026** as the date the displayed consolidation began to apply.

The PISRS record carries an express warning that an unofficial consolidated text is only an informational working aid and that the authority gives no guarantee or liability for it. Use NPB 14 as a navigation and working-text reference. The underlying promulgated acts in the Official Gazette remain the legal publication sources if exact wording, amendment effect or a discrepancy matters.

ZUP-I was published in Official Gazette RS 85/2025 on 6 November 2025. Article 87 provides that it enters into force three months after publication; PISRS identifies the NPB 14 use date as 7 February 2026. It makes broad changes to the general procedure. Among the expressly published changes are electronic filings and service, an informative decision with an objection mechanism, and a mechanism for waiving judicial protection. The amendment also has transitional rules and staged commencement: for example, Article 85 delays application of the amended service rule in §87 for one year after commencement, subject to an exception. Check the specific amendment and transition provision relevant to the procedural event.

## Using this reference in a live matter

1. Identify the public body, the type of decision and whether the matter is an administrative matter under ZUP.
2. Locate the special statute for that substantive field and identify which procedure rules it changes or replaces.
3. Confirm which ZUP version applies to the event, especially where a proceeding, service, appeal period or other step crosses 7 February 2026 or a staged commencement date.
4. Read the decision's remedy notice and the operative statute for the actual appeal or court deadline. This general reference does not establish a case-specific forum, deadline, suspension effect or remedy.
