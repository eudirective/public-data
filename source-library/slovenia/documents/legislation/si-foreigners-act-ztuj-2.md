## Scope

ZTuj-2 is Slovenia's statutory framework for entry, departure and residence of foreign nationals. The Act also contains provisions relevant to residence of EU citizens and their family members; the applicable EU, EEA, Swiss, Slovenian-national-family and third-country routes must be distinguished by the provision and legal basis in issue.
