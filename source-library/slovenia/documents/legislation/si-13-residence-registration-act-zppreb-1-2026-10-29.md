## Scope

The Residence Registration Act (Zakon o prijavi prebivališča, ZPPreb-1) regulates registration and deregistration duties for permanent and temporary residence in Slovenia, permanent and temporary addresses abroad, service addresses, the permanent population register, temporary-accommodation records, guest registration and guest records.

The statute concerns residence-address registration and public records. It should be kept separate from residence permits and substantive rights of residence under immigration, EU free-movement or international law. A person's registration status under this Act does not, by itself, establish entitlement to reside under those regimes.

## Structure and operational boundaries

The PISRS text is organized into general provisions; permanent and temporary residence and addresses abroad; residence fact-finding and refusal; service addresses; the permanent population register; temporary-accommodation and guest records; supervision; penalties; and transitional/final provisions.

The Act is relevant to residence registration, address changes, official recordkeeping and specified data access or processing. It is not a complete account of the administrative steps for every person or accommodation arrangement. Confirm the current provision, the person's status, the competent administrative unit and any applicable implementing regulation before acting.

## Related sources and limitations

For a foreign national, check this Act alongside the Foreigners Act and the relevant EU/EEA, Swiss or other residence framework. Registration of an address and immigration permission answer different legal questions. For EU citizens and family members, consult the specific residence-rights and registration-certificate rules as well as this address-registration statute.
