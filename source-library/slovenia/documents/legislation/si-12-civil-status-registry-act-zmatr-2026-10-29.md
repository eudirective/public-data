## Scope

The Civil Status Registry Act (Zakon o matičnem registru, ZMatR) governs the civil-status register: its content, competent authorities and operation, and the collection and provision of register information. The register records births, marriages or partnerships, deaths and other facts specified by law. It also provides for registering relevant events that occurred abroad for Slovenian citizens, issuing extracts and certificates, correcting register errors, and access to personal data under statutory conditions.

The PISRS text identifies administrative units as competent for entries. The Act is therefore a core reference for recording civil-status events and obtaining official register extracts. The specific evidence required, competent unit, correction route and access entitlement depend on the event and statutory provisions. This note does not assess an individual registration or document request.

## Subject areas

The Act is organized around general provisions, entries of events occurring in Slovenia, entries of events occurring abroad, register administration and data provision, supervision, penalties and transitional/final provisions. The PISRS text indicates that the register supports entries for births, marriage or partnership, deaths and other legally specified facts; it also regulates extracts and certificates, corrections, retention and access.

For an event abroad, consult the Act's foreign-event provisions and any applicable treaty or EU rules. The Act's register-entry rules do not alone decide recognition or substantive validity of a foreign civil-status act. Keep civil-status registration distinct from the substantive family-law rules governing marriage, parentage or adoption, and from nationality or residence consequences.
