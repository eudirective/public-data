# Finland source-route sustainability assessment

Review date: 21 October 2026
State: 36 candidate records; eight accepted official external law references; zero accepted local copies; country review incomplete.

## Decision

The direct Finlex current-law pages are sustainable as external source references. Finlex is owned by the Ministry of Justice, maintained by the Legal Register Centre, marks consolidated legislation current, and exposes stable instrument URLs and versioned ELI identifiers. The accepted links are listed in `source-acceptance.json`.

A local API capture or conversion workflow is optional. Finlex documents a public Akoma Ntoso API and downloadable packages, but no live payload or converter has been verified. That leaves local-copy maintenance unimplemented; it does not make the direct government-published consolidated pages unusable or unsustainable as references. Do not hold legal-source acceptance behind registration, XML replay, custom conversion, or reconstruction of Gazette fragments.

## Limits and dependencies

- This pack accepts the specified Finnish and Swedish Finlex page references, not local copies or extracted legal text. The research viewer could not return the full Aliens Act body due to its size, so no line-by-line semantic comparison is claimed.
- The Aliens Act's current Finnish ELI page was dated 26 June 2026. Amendment 912/2026 was published 20 October and takes effect 27 October; the page should be rechecked after that date. The amendment changes work-based permit provisions in §§ 81a, 82 and 84, not Chapter 10 EU residence provisions.
- Current-version ELI dates for the eight accepted law references are recorded in the acceptance file: seven accepted Finnish pages and the current Swedish page for the Police Personal Data Act. Its Finnish page needs a post-amendment refresh. The currentness check is a dated observation, not an automated alert.
- The English Aliens Act translation ends at amendment 389/2023. English procedural translations are also behind current Finnish law. Finnish text is the accepted reference where translation versions are stale.
- Finnish and Swedish are both official statutory languages. The Swedish Aliens Act page surfaced at 13 June 2025 while the Finnish consolidation is dated 26 June 2026; the Swedish Data Protection Act page surfaced at 27 June 2025 while the Finnish consolidation is dated 22 May 2026. The Swedish Openness Act version date was not surfaced. Confirm each parallel-language version before current-law reliance.
- For the Police Personal Data Act, the Swedish current page is dated 4 September 2026 and includes amendments 728/2026 and 830/2026 effective 1 October. The Finnish version last surfaced at 9 July 2026; refresh it against those amendments.
- A current Finlex link is not a finding that the legal topics, remedies, operational practices or country pack are complete.

## Site-level human review

Official government and court websites for Migri, the Ministry for Foreign Affairs, DVV, the courts, Oikeus.fi, oversight bodies and the National Legal Services Authority are listed in `README.md` and `source-acceptance.json`. These whole-site links give the reviewer a usable starting point while exact candidate pages, mission routes, forms, case-law publication limits and complaint processes are reviewed. A viewer restriction on a page is recorded as a client limitation, not source unavailability.
