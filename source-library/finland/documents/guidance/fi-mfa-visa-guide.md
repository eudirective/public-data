## Source role

This official Ministry page is the English guidance route identified in the Finland source register for short-stay visa information and the visa route relevant to family members covered by EU free-movement rules. It is administrative guidance, not legislation and not a complete statement of legal rights or every mission’s practice.

For legal questions, consult the current Finnish and EU instruments and the applicable individual decision. The library’s [Finnish Aliens Act](../legislation/fi-aliens-act-301-2004-current-fi.md) is the national statutory reference. Follow the live MFA page’s links to country-specific mission instructions and application services when those are needed.

The page is maintained by the MFA and may change. Fees, deadlines, appointment channels, document requirements, and mission-specific directions must be checked on the live publisher page and the relevant mission or application portal at the time of use. The MFA says its Finnish-language pages provide the most comprehensive information.
