# Finland source references

Review date: 21 October 2026. This is a partial, link-based source pack. Eight consolidated-law page references from Finlex are accepted as official external references: seven use the current Finnish text, while the Police Personal Data Act reference uses the current Swedish text pending a refresh of its Finnish page. No local legal-text copies or conversions are accepted. The 36-record candidate inventory remains open; 28 records still need individual acceptance or human review, and Finland is not marked complete.

Finlex identifies itself as a public legal-information service owned by Finland's Ministry of Justice and maintained by the Legal Register Centre. It labels consolidated statute pages as current and provides versioned ELI identifiers. The direct current-law pages are sufficient references for this pack; API access, XML conversion, and Gazette reconstruction are not prerequisites. Finlex also links the original promulgated statute and amendment history. Compare those where an exact discrepancy or transitional question matters.

## Currentness and language notes

- Act 360/2007 is an amending act; the current consolidated Aliens Act is the operative source record for its amendments.
- The official English Aliens Act translation stops at amendments through 389/2023. The English Administrative Procedure Act translation is also old, and the English administrative-court procedure translation predates amendment 213/2026. Do not use those translations as current text.
- Finland publishes legislation in Finnish and Swedish. The current-version page must be checked in both languages: the Swedish Aliens Act page surfaced at 13 June 2025, older than the Finnish 26 June 2026 page; the Swedish Data Protection Act page surfaced at 27 June 2025, older than the Finnish 22 May 2026 page. The Swedish Openness Act version date was not surfaced in this review.
- Amendment 912/2026 was published on 20 October and takes effect on 27 October 2026. It amends Aliens Act §§ 81a, 82 and 84 concerning work-based residence-permit processing; the text does not amend Chapter 10. Recheck the consolidated Aliens Act after commencement.
- The 808/2019 source note that gave 29 June 2021 as the current version was stale. Finnish and Swedish Finlex pages now identify the 27 March 2026 consolidation. For the Police Personal Data Act, the accepted current page is Swedish; the Finnish page needs a post-amendment refresh. The same date is visible for both languages for the Administrative Procedure Act, Language Act and Legal Aid Act.

## Official sites offered for human review

These government and court websites contain candidate guidance or procedures that have not been accepted as individual text records. Review the whole site or the linked category pages as useful; a site-level link is not acceptance of every page on that site.

- [Finnish Immigration Service (Migri)](https://migri.fi/en/) — EU registration, family residence cards, applications, fees and processing times. The direct registration page returned 403 to one research viewer; that is a viewer restriction, not evidence that the source is unavailable.
- [Ministry for Foreign Affairs of Finland](https://um.fi/) — visa guidance, consular instructions and mission routes.
- [Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV)](https://dvv.fi/en) — population registration and municipality-of-residence procedures.
- [Finnish courts portal](https://www.tuomioistuimet.fi/en/) and [Oikeus.fi](https://www.oikeus.fi/en/) — court procedures, appeals and public-access guidance.
- [Parliamentary Ombudsman](https://www.oikeusasiamies.fi/en), [Chancellor of Justice](https://oikeuskansleri.fi/en/) and [Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/home) — distinct oversight routes.
- [National Legal Services Authority](https://www.oikeuspalveluvirasto.fi/en/) — public legal-aid guidance.
- [N-Lex](https://n-lex.europa.eu/n-lex/) — discovery directory for Finland's national legal database; verify items at their first-party publisher.
