## Local research archive

The local-only archive is outside this library overview; the live Monitor report includes its separate count. Archive records are excluded from the library source total.

### Counting rules

- Scope is all catalogued statuses in the 31 national packs plus the active shared EU core manifest.
- Existing pack entry IDs are retained. External references, local artifacts and acceptance states link to a canonical `source_identity_id`.
- Representations never increase the source total. Exact matches to shared-core CELEX identities belong to shared EU core and are counted once.
- Distinct catalogue IDs with an unresolved identity match remain separate provisional identities and are listed above. The total is exact only when no unresolved identity remains.
- A stale mirror is a separate snapshot from GitHub; it cannot support an exact-current GitHub total.
- The Monitor local research archive is reported separately and is never merged into the library total.
