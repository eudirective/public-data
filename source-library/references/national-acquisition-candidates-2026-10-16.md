## Acquisition handling

- Retrieve legal texts from the official national publisher, preserving the promulgated text and any separate official consolidated view; record the effective-date/version used.
- Treat official administrative pages as operational evidence, not substitutes for legislation or appeal rules.
- Resolve every I item’s exact publisher record, currentness and scope before retrieval; do not let one candidate stand in for an entire taxonomy category.
- Add accepted country material only in its country pack with source identity, retrieval metadata, conversion review and explicit acceptance status.
- Shared EU materials remain in the EU core; check for duplicate source identities before adding language or national representations.
