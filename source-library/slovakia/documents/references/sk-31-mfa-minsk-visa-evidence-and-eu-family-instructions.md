## Scope

This is the Embassy in Minsk's country-specific Schengen visa evidence page. It contains a general checklist and a separate statement for family members of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens. It is not a national rulebook and does not establish requirements at another Slovak mission or visa centre.

## EU/EEA/Swiss family members

The page expressly says that its general document checklist does **not** apply to family members of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and that their applications are handled under a simplified regime. It separately warns that family members of Slovak citizens are not treated as EU-family members for visa purposes, except for statutory exceptions.

Read those statements with current Slovak law. Act 404/2011 §2(5) defines relevant family-member categories, including an exception for certain third-country family members of Slovak citizens returning from another Member State; §7 governs entry; and §16(17) lists the travel document, family-link evidence and dependency evidence where required for an EU-family Schengen visa application. It sets a maximum decision period of 10 days. Article 5(2) of Directive 2004/38/EC requires qualifying visas to be issued free of charge, as soon as possible, under an accelerated procedure.

The general checklist elsewhere on the Minsk page includes additional evidence such as travel insurance, financial means, return travel, a police-verified invitation for certain nationalities, and local application arrangements. The page says the list does not apply to EU-family applicants. Do not import those standard requirements into an EU-family case without checking the applicable law and competent authority. The family-specific legal category and the route of the Union citizen still need to be established.

## General checklist and Minsk-specific process

For applicants to whom it applies, the page describes a completed visa form, passport, photograph, travel medical insurance, and supporting documents tied to the applicant's employment, business, studies, pension, family situation, lawful stay in Belarus and travel purpose. It gives a stated daily amount for financial support and permits the Embassy to request additional information or an interview. The page cautions that submission of the checklist does not guarantee a visa.

The page lists booking links for BLS International and VFS Global, says applicants generally appear in person for fingerprints, and gives exceptions for specified categories. It says the page applies to nationals of a listed group of countries, including Belarus, who are asked for a police-verified invitation regardless of travel purpose. These operational instructions are date-sensitive. Because the EU-family section excludes those applicants from the general list, verify any interaction between the general invitation requirement and a specific EU-family case directly with the competent authority and current law.

## Currentness and limits

The page displays an update date of 12 February 2026 and was directly opened on 29 October 2026. It is accepted as a current-dated official reference to what the Minsk Embassy page states within its local scope. It is not a guarantee that a provider link, appointment route, fee, list item or local practice is still operating on the date of a future application.

Do not generalize the Minsk process to all applicants in Belarus or to other missions without confirming consular competence. The Embassy itself provides contact for submission questions. The linked provider, availability, and the applicant's lawful place of residence should be checked at time of use. This note is not an application checklist or legal conclusion.
