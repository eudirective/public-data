## What the Hanoi page says

The Embassy page concerns entry by EU/EEA/Swiss citizens and their family members, and an entry-visa application by a family member who is a third-country national. It describes evidence of identity and nationality or family relationship, and dependency evidence where relevant. It says these visa applications are free and are decided through an accelerated procedure “within 10 working days.”

The page lists spouses; children under 21 or dependent children; dependent direct ascendants; certain other dependent, household or health-dependent family members; and a duly certified durable partner. It also discusses a third-country national related to a Slovak citizen returning to Slovakia after residence in another EU Member State. Treat this as the scope of the 2022 page, not a complete statement of current statutory coverage.

## Currentness and legal reconciliation

This is an old mission-specific page, not current national-law text. It cites Regulation (EC) No 562/2006 as the Schengen Borders Code. That Regulation was repealed and replaced by Regulation (EU) 2016/399. The page does not display a later revision date.

The current Slovak consolidated Act 404/2011 version effective 1 October 2026 is the controlling domestic reference for the routes reviewed here. Section 7(17) says that a third-country family member of a Union citizen must provide a valid travel document, proof of the family relationship, and dependency evidence when required; it states that the diplomatic mission decides no later than **10 days**. The Hanoi page’s “10 working days” wording does not match that current statutory wording. Do not rely on the page’s deadline formulation; check the current law and the competent mission.

The page’s statement that the visa is free is consistent with Article 5(2) of Directive 2004/38/EC, which requires the necessary visa for a qualifying non-EU family member to be issued free of charge, as soon as possible, under an accelerated procedure. This does not make every person described on the page automatically eligible; the applicable family-member definition and the EU citizen’s route must be checked.

The mission page is accepted only as evidence of what the Embassy in Hanoi published on 7 July 2022 and as a discovery lead to verify the current mission process. It is **not accepted as current operational guidance**, a substitute for Act 404/2011, or proof of the current Hanoi appointment channel, submission process, document checklist, or processing time. In particular, verify whether Hanoi remains the competent mission for the applicant and whether any external service provider is used.
