- **Language:** Slovak
- **Published and updated:** 17 September 2025
- **Reviewed:** 29 October 2026
- **Source class:** Mission-specific EU-family visa guidance
- **Record type:** Maintained official-reference note; not a source-text copy

## Who the page covers

The page explains the EU-family visa route for certain third-country-national family members accompanying or joining an EU citizen in Slovakia. It gives categories including a spouse; specified children; dependent direct relatives; other family members dependent in the country of origin, in the EU citizen's household, or dependent for serious health reasons; and a partner with a durable, duly attested relationship. It also describes a limited route for a third-country family member of a Slovak citizen returning to or rejoining that citizen in Slovakia after residence in another Member State.

The page extends its described conditions to family members of UK citizens who rely on the Withdrawal Agreement and join the UK citizen in the host state after the transition period. This is a distinct, treaty-based route; do not apply it to every UK national or family relationship without checking the Withdrawal Agreement conditions and current Slovak law.

## Application material and process described

For a Schengen visa application under this route, the page lists:

1. A completed and signed application form. A legal representative signs for a minor or legally incapable applicant and attaches evidence of that authority. The page says the online application may be used and that the mission may require electronic completion.
2. A valid travel document.
3. One ICAO-compliant colour photograph, 3.5 × 4.5 cm, on a light background.
4. Fingerprints when required. The page states they are generally collected once in each 59-month period and describes exceptions for children under 12, physical impossibility, and certain official delegations.
5. Supporting evidence of the Union citizen's identity and nationality; that the applicant will accompany or join that person in Slovakia; and the family relationship or, where required, dependency.

The page states that qualifying EU-family visas are issued as soon as possible and no later than 10 days after the application, and that the application is free of charge.

## Legal cross-check and important distinctions

The current Slovak Act 404/2011 version effective 1 October 2026 provides the relevant domestic framework. Sections 2, 7 and 16(17) cover family-member definitions and exceptions, entry conditions, supporting evidence and the 10-day decision period for an EU-family Schengen visa. Article 5(2) of Directive 2004/38/EC requires qualifying visas to be issued free of charge, as soon as possible, under an accelerated procedure.

The mission page labels Switzerland under an “EFTA” heading in its list of EEA countries. Switzerland is not a contracting party to the EEA Agreement. Slovak law separately includes the Swiss Confederation in the relevant member-state definition for these rules; preserve that distinction when classifying a case.

The page's definition of an EU citizen excludes Slovak citizens but describes statutory exceptions for a returning Slovak citizen's family. Check whether the applicant fits the precise exception in Act 404/2011 §2(5)(h), rather than treating all family members of Slovak citizens as covered by the ordinary EU-family route.

The separate Minsk general-visa evidence page, updated 12 February 2026, says its general checklist does not apply to EU/EEA/Swiss family members. Do not import ordinary Schengen requirements into this route without a legal basis. That general page and this family-specific page concern Minsk practice only.

## Scope and limits

The official page displayed an update date of 17 September 2025 and was directly opened on 29 October 2026. It is accepted as a Minsk-specific official description of the application materials and process stated on that page. It does not establish the current competent mission for a particular applicant, the available appointment channel, provider use, or practices across the Slovak mission network. The online form requirement, biometric arrangements and local submission details may change; verify them with the competent mission before use.

This note is an authored summary, not a copy of the page or a legal conclusion on eligibility. No topic or country-completion finding follows from this single mission reference.
