- Thirty-five locator/disposition notes record accepted official links, review scope, candidate provenance and use limits. They do not reproduce captured page content.

Unaccepted captures remain in the EU Source Update Monitor's indexed local-only archive. Link acceptance, retained text, legal currentness, topic coverage and country completion are separate states.
