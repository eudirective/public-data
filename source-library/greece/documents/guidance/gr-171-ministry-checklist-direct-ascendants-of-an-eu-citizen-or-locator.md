## Source role and accepted scope

Initial documentary checklist for a third-country-national direct ascendant of a Union citizen or the citizen’s spouse/partner under PD 106/2007 category 5.3.5. Historical checklist text only; not current requirements or a complete procedure.

## Review findings

The one-page Greek-language file requests an application form; four recent physical colour photographs plus JPEG2000 digital photographs on CD; a €16 printing fee; valid passport or recognized travel document; the residence card of the Union-citizen family member or a Schengen C visa where required; health-insurance evidence; the Union citizen’s registration certificate; civil-status evidence of the relationship; and documented material dependence, prior household membership or serious-health/personal-care evidence. Its insurance paragraph cites Article 136(3) of Law 4251/2014, consistent with its historical status.
