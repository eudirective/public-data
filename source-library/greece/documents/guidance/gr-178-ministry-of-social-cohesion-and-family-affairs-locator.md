## Source role and accepted scope

Official government publication of an attributed ministerial statement on the general duration of social investigations for foster care and adoption; contextual evidence only, not enacted law.

## Review findings

The Minister states that legislation provides for social investigations to be completed within three months for foster care and six months for adoption, and says some regional services miss these periods. The page gives no legal citation or start event for the six-month adoption period. Final Gazette Law 4704/2020 Article 24 (gr-107) verifies a six-month report period for a specific-child application with a distinct filing/service trigger and scope; it does not identify the trigger for this broader ministerial statement or establish AnyNet’s pre-matching worker-assignment clock. The related Law 4538/2018 Article 26 chain (Articles 26(1), as amended by Law 4611/2019 Article 92 and Law 5107/2024 Article 7) includes Civil Code Article 1557 but points reassigned-worker reporting to Articles 10 and 11, whose original text concerns foster-care scenarios; this does not identify the trigger for the Minister’s broad statement or establish the AnyNet assignment-based timer.
