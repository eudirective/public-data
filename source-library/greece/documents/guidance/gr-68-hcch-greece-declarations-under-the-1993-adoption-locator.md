## Source role and accepted scope

Greece-specific declarations/notifications under Articles 15, 22 and 25 of the 1993 Convention, including the stated conditions for recognition in Greece of a Convention adoption from another Contracting State.

## Review findings

The notification record was opened. It describes delegated Central Authority functions, the public-authority/accredited-body rule for adoptions of children habitually resident in Greece, Greece’s Article 25 declaration, and the certificate plus public-order/best-interests conditions stated for recognition.
