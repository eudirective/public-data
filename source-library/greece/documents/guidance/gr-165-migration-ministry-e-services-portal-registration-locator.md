## Source role and accepted scope

Account registration instructions for third-country residence-document holders and adult third-country nationals with a grant/renewal electronic application already protocolled by the competent service. The page identifies the residence-card and permanent-residence-card types for family members of Greek or EU citizens. It is not an initial-application checklist or filing-route specification.

## Review findings

The official help page says access to electronic services follows registration. It gives registration categories, distinguishes residence-document types including the residence card and permanent residence card held by family members of Greek/EU citizens, and permits the alternative “electronic application” identifier only where a grant/renewal application has already been protocolled and remains pending. It describes identity data, mobile one-time code and account setup. The official portal login page was also opened and displayed login fields plus registration, password-recovery, instructions and contact links.
