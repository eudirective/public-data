# gr-223 — Ministry Circular No. 50/2011 on EU residence evidence in naturalisation cases

- Official document: [Circular No. 50/2011 PDF](https://old.synigoros.gr/resources/docs/egkyklios50-2011.pdf)
- Publisher: Hellenic Ministry of Interior, General Secretariat for Population and Social Cohesion. The Ministry is identified in the document header; the PDF is hosted in the Greek Ombudsman’s legacy archive.
- Identity: Circular No. 50, protocol F.130181/42077, dated 9 November 2011; 14 pages.
- Reviewed scope: Section A concerns evidence of lawful residence for EU citizens applying for Greek naturalisation under the then-applicable three-year residence rule. The Ministry describes the EU registration certificate as evidentiary rather than constitutive and allows other evidence of lawful residence. Section B addresses continuity of residence titles and says gaps shorter than three months did not interrupt the specified historical calculation. The appendix reproduces selected provisions of the original PD 106/2007.
- Classification: official historical administrative interpretation for naturalisation. This is not a current naturalisation checklist, general residence ruling, or residence-card procedure.
- Limits: subsequent citizenship and residence-law changes are not reviewed here. Do not use the circular to determine current eligibility or present evidentiary requirements. The subject is EU citizens applying for naturalisation, not the TCN family-member residence card. Greek is controlling; no local source text or automated retrieval rule is accepted.
- Reviewed: 29 October 2026. No source text is reproduced in this locator.
