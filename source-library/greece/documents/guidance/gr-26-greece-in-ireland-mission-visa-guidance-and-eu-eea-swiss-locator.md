## Review findings

Applicants submit in person by appointment; the page says EU family members should also book and identify themselves. Its EU-family section lists three screening conditions: the EU citizen exercises free movement, the applicant is a qualifying family member, and the applicant accompanies or joins that citizen. On refusal, written reasons are given and the refusal form supplies appeal information.
