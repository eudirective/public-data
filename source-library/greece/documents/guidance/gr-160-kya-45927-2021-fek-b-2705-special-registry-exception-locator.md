## Source role and accepted scope

Original KYA 45927/2021 Article 1 categories for exceptional transcription by the Athens Special Registry under Law 4674/2020 Article 31.

## Review findings

The exact instrument number, title, legal basis and issue date were checked in the official Gazette. Its sole article lists six exception groups: records requiring recognition of a foreign judgment; events not timely declared at the competent consulate where the people concerned now reside permanently in Greece or another country than the event country; travel exceeding 500 kilometres to the competent consulate or no consulate; corrections/certifications under a final Greek court judgment; consular inability due to a force-majeure suspension; and events within honorary-consulate jurisdiction with listed filing/transmission options. The instrument states it takes effect upon publication.
