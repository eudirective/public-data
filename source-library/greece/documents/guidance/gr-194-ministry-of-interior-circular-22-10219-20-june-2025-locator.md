## Source role and accepted scope

Official notice and Ministry reproduction of the amendment made by Decision 8229/16-5-2025, FEK B 2601/27-5-2025, to Article 2 of Decision 21797/2024.

## Review findings

The complete circular identifies the amendment decision and Gazette issue, then reproduces the replacement Article 2 text. It states that a named request includes the applicant name, surname, identity-card number and contact details, whether filed in person or electronically; an email-submitted named application must be an attached signed document using a qualified electronic signature or signature verification through the specified gov.gr digital-document applications. This is an official Ministry circular reproduction, not the Gazette amendment itself.
