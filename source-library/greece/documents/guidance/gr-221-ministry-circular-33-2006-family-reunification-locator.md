## Reviewed scope

The full document was opened and reviewed. It explains Presidential Decree 131/2006 implementing Directive 2003/86/EC for family reunification of lawfully resident third-country nationals. It explicitly excludes family members of Union citizens from that route and points to the then-specific national framework. The circular discusses application processing, refusal, withdrawal/non-renewal, family-impact factors, reasons and historical remedy routes.

## Disposition and limits

Accepted as a historical Ministry circular by link only. It concerns the TCN family-reunification route under Directive 2003/86/EC, not EU free movement or residence cards for family members of Union citizens. It predates subsequent migration codes and amendments; its requirements, institutions, deadlines and references to the former Law 3068/2002 route must not be used as current law. It does not establish current Greek procedure.
