## Review findings

The Hellenic DPA legal-framework page links this PDF as the law with all amendments integrated. The reviewed text includes the change to Article 6(5) attributed to Law 5002/2022 Article 34 and the repeal of Article 11(6) attributed to Law 5043/2023 Article 123. On 26 October 2026, the official 67-page DPA-hosted text was directly reviewed at Chapter D Articles 43, 45A, 55, 56, 58 and 60. Article 43 limits that Chapter to competent-authority processing for criminal-law/public-security purposes and assigns other-purpose processing to the GDPR where applicable and Chapters A–C. Article 45A requires a Union or national legal basis and necessity and says specific legal rules must define purpose, data, safeguards and competent authorities. Article 55 provides access information including purpose/legal basis, recipients, retention and rights/remedies; Article 56 provides correction/completion, deletion or restriction routes and refusal safeguards; Article 58 permits an HDPA complaint and identifies a Council of State annulment action against the HDPA complaint decision; Article 60 places rights and compensation claims against the controller where a processor acts on its behalf. These are observations from the DPA-hosted text under a June 2023 path, not a 2026 current consolidation.
