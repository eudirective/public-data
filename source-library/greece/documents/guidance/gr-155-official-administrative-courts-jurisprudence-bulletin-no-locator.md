## Source role and accepted scope

Official indexed summaries for four specific administrative-court disputes: residence-permit revocation (Patras), detention pending removal (Corfu), long-term-resident refusal/return (Larissa) and EU-family residence after divorce (Thessaloniki). Accepted only as abstract-level references; underlying judgments are not accepted.

## Review findings

The Patras abstract (ΔΠΡ Πατρών ΑΔ12/2024) concerns annulment of revocation of a ten-year residence permit under Law 4251/2014 Articles 6(c), 24 and 138. It says prior convictions are not an automatic ground; public-order assessment must address a real, present and sufficiently serious threat and weigh offense severity, residence duration and personal/family ties consistently with proportionality. It reports that the challenged decision was annulled and remitted for a new lawful assessment. The Corfu abstract (ΔΠΡ Κέρκυρας ΑΡ15/2025) concerns detention pending removal under Law 3907/2011 Article 30(2) and Law 3386/2005 Article 76(3)–(5); it describes rejection of arguments about prior hearing, proportionality, good administration and presumption of innocence, but the accessible indexed extract is truncated before a final order. The Larissa abstract (ΔΠΡ Λάρισας ΑΔ9/2025) concerns refusal of long-term-resident status on renewal of an employment permit plus a return order without a voluntary-departure period. It cites Law 4251/2014 Articles 6, 24, 90 and 92 and summarizes a requirement for reasoned individualized assessment of public-order risk; the final order is not visible. The Thessaloniki abstract (ΔΠΡ Θεσσαλονίκης ΑΔ30/2025) concerns a third-country family member’s residence after divorce under PD 106/2007 Article 12(2), individualized review and a prior-hearing issue; its final order is not visible.
