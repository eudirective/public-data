## Source role and accepted scope

Dated Ministry statement that Greece began issuing electronic unified-format residence permits in early November 2020 and that subsequently issued residence permits and residence cards would use the then-current format; older-format documents remained valid until renewal.

## Review findings

The Ministry says that, in cooperation with the Hellenic Police Headquarters, it began issuing the electronic card in early November 2020 under the uniform permit framework (the page renders the instrument number/year as 1954/2017, i.e. Regulation (EU) 2017/1954 amending Regulation (EC) No 1030/2002). It states that all residence permits and residence cards issued thereafter would use the new format, while earlier documents remained valid until renewal. It describes an RFID chip containing the holder’s facial image, fingerprints and signature. The linked image was not visually inspected.
