## Source role and accepted scope

Original dated requirements in KYA 95391/2024 for third-country-sponsor family-reunification entry approval and national-visa stages under Law 5038/2023 Articles 84–85, and selected residence-document applications for third-country family members of Union citizens under PD 106/2007, including direct ascendants. The dated document-list scope also includes KYA Article 59(8)–(9): standard permanent-residence cards and the Article 14 derogation cases for third-country family members.

## Review findings

The Gazette PDF identifies KYA 95391/2024, issue B 1807 and date. Article 23 (PDF page 16) lists the family-reunification approval file under Law 5038/2023 Articles 84–85, the national-visa stage (approved dossier sent by the competent one-stop service to the consulate; general D-visa documents are delegated to a separate decision), and the listed visa and residence-permit fees. Pages 34–37 list third-country family-member documents under PD 106/2007. Article 59(5)–(6) specifically lists initial and renewal documents for direct ascendants of a Union citizen or that citizen’s spouse/partner; pages 36–37 also cover selected Article 3(2) categories and durable partners. On 26 October 2026, the Gazette text at PDF pages 36–37 was reviewed for Article 59(8)–(9). Paragraph (8) lists a valid residence card and active health-insurance capacity or private insurance for a standard permanent-residence card. Paragraph (9), for the PD 106/2007 Article 14 derogations, additionally lists the Union citizen’s registration/permanent-residence document, recent proof that the family relationship continues and, in specified death cases, evidence of the relevant Greek residence period or occupational cause of death.
