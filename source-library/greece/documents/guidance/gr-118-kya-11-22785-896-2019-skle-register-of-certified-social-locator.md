## Source role and accepted scope

Original joint ministerial decision on the SKLE special register of certified social workers trained in adoption and foster-care matters, including social investigations under Law 4538/2018 Article 26.

## Review findings

The 8-page Gazette PDF identifies FEK B 2198/7.6.2019 and the decision number/title. Articles 1–4 describe a regional electronic register, professional/training prerequisites, application and ranking, and free 30-hour training for social workers who conduct suitability investigations for prospective adoptive or foster parents.
