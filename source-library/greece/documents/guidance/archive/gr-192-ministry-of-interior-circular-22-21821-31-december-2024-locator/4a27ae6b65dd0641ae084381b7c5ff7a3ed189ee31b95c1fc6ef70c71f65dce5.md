## Source role and accepted scope

Official explanatory circular on Law 5143/2024 Article 59 replacing Article 5 of Law 2690/1999, effective 1 January 2025.

## Review findings

The complete four-page Ministry circular identifies itself and explains the Article 59 changes: removal of prior reasonable-interest/special-legal-interest thresholds subject to third-party personal data; electronic and anonymous requests; partial disclosure and online referral; vagueness/repetition limits; functional scope for covered bodies; assistance in locating documents; written-request form and 20-day response rule. This is Ministry guidance summarizing the statute, not the statute or a consolidated Code.
