## Source role and accepted scope

Original Article 1(4) amendment to Ministerial Decision 7004/3/40-θ and Article 2 commencement only. It assigns Article 77 Law 3386/2005 appeal decisions and Article 78 force-majeure deportation suspension decisions to the named police bodies as at 1 January 2006.

## Review findings

The eight-page official-government-hosted Gazette issue identifies series B, issue 1730, dated 9 December 2005 and bears the National Printing House imprint. Article 1(4) adds case ΙΘ, assigning decisions on appeals against deportation under Article 77 of Law 3386/2005 and force-majeure suspension under Article 78 to the regional general police directors and directors of the Attica and Thessaloniki Aliens Directorates. Article 2 states that the decision takes effect on 1 January 2006.
