## Review findings

Indexed official Gov.gr content says apply within three months of entry to the migration service for the place of residence; present ID or a valid travel document and a stated EUR 16 printing fee; receive a blue acknowledgment when documents are complete; expect a decision within six months of a complete application; and receive appeal authority/deadline information under PD 106/2007 Article 24.
