## Review findings

The official Gazette reproduction identifies FEK B 3149/19 July 2021 and the decision P23MET-34908. Its operative clauses establish the Certified Translators Register, specify its alphabetical ordering and listed information fields, and require publication on the MFA website with a link to the gov.gr translator-search application. The linked later decision P23MET-38227/30 July 2021 (FEK B 3482) could not be opened in this review and is not accepted here.
