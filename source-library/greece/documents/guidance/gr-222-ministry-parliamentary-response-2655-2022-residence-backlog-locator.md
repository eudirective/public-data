# gr-222 — Ministry response to Parliamentary Question 2655/2022

- Official document: [Ministry response PDF](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/UserFiles/67715b2c-ec81-4f0c-ad6a-476a34d732bd/11878750.pdf)
- Publisher: Hellenic Ministry of Migration and Asylum, response to the Hellenic Parliament.
- Date and identity: ten-page response dated 23 February 2022 to Question 2655/24 January 2022 on pending residence applications under Law 4251/2014 and PD 106/2007.
- Reviewed scope: the Ministry explains Article 139 of Law 4876/2021, which treated applications submitted by 31 December 2020 as accepted without an individual decision and counted time through 31 March 2022 as lawful residence. It describes the pandemic backlog, transition to electronic renewals, and tables of pending cases by office and residence-title type. Annex 2 lists five EU-family ascendant renewals under PD 106/2007 Article 9.
- Classification: historical governmental implementation account and administrative statistics. It is not legislation, an individual decision, or current procedural guidance.
- Limits: the measure was time-limited and is expired. Most cases/statistics concern third-country national routes; the five Article 9 entries do not establish broader EU-family practice. Counts are a February 2022 snapshot. Greek is controlling. No source text is reproduced here.
- Retrieval: opened and reviewed on 29 October 2026. No automated retrieval rule or local source copy accepted.
