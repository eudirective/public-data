## Review findings

Hellenic Police page dated 11 May 2020 states that EU/EEA/Swiss citizens need not declare stays up to three months, describes its valid-document condition and an additional three months for actual job-seekers, and cites PD 106/2007 Article 6(1) as amended by Law 4071/2012 Article 42.
