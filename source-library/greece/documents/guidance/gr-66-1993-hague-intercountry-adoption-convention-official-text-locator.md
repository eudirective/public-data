## Source role and accepted scope

Official English text of the 1993 Hague Convention for intercountry adoptions within Article 2; treaty safeguards and recognition effects only. This is not Greek domestic adoption legislation or a general source on recognition of foreign family judgments.

## Review findings

The official text was opened. Articles 23–25 address recognition of a Convention-compliant adoption certified by the competent authority, the limited public-policy exception considering the child’s best interests, and a declaration concerning Article 39(2) agreements.
