## Source role and accepted scope

Official parliamentary analysis of the proposed extension of civil marriage and its interaction with Civil Code adoption Articles 1544–1546 and 1562. Pages 7–9 interpret the bill as enabling same-sex married couples to jointly adopt a minor and to adopt a spouse’s child, subject to applicable parental-consent rules. Does not establish current consolidated text or procedure.

## Review findings

The 24-page Scientific Service report is dated 13 February 2024. Pages 7–9 describe the adoption provisions and interpret Article 3 of the bill later enacted as Law 5089/2024: the report states that same-sex spouses may jointly adopt a minor and that a spouse may adopt the other spouse’s child, subject to consent by the other natural parent where applicable under Civil Code Articles 1550 and 1552. It says the bill does not amend Civil Code Articles 1542 onward and treats the adoption effect as a consequence of extending civil marriage to same-sex couples. The enacted Law 5089/2024 Gazette is separately accepted at gr-76.
