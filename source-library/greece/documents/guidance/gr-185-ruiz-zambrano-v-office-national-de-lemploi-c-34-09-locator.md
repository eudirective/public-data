## Review findings

Official EUR-Lex judgment identity and date verified; procedural posture: Preliminary reference from the Brussels Labour Tribunal; Grand Chamber. Screened holding: Article 20 TFEU precludes refusing residence and a work permit to a dependent third-country parent of minor Union-citizen children where those refusals would require the children in practice to leave the Union and thereby deprive them of the genuine enjoyment of the substance of Union-citizen rights.
