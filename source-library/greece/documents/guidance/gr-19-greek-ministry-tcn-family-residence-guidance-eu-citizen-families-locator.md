## Review findings

On 26 October 2026 the reader exposed the page through its substantive sections. It states a three-month post-arrival application instruction and lists initial/renewal documents for spouses or partners, direct descendants (including adopted children), direct ascendants, other family members and stable partners; it also lists evidence sections for an individual right of residence and permanent residence. The other-family section addresses dependency, prior household or serious-health-care grounds and reciprocal dependency; the stable-partner section lists evidence examples including cohabitation, a common child/adoption, and long-term legal, social or economic ties. The individual-retention section lists documents associated with death or departure, children in education, separation/divorce or partnership dissolution, domestic violence, difficult circumstances and contact with a minor child. The shared-document section still lists four physical photographs, JPEG2000 on CD and a EUR 16 fee, and linked text refers to Law 4251/2014. The page has no visible update date.
