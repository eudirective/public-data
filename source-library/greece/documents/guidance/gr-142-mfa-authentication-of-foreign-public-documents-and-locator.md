## Source role and accepted scope

Dated official guidance on authentication of foreign public documents for Greek authorities, including the sequence before official translation, general Apostille/consular authentication routes, and certified-translator information.

## Review findings

The page states that authentication of foreign public documents must precede official translation. It describes the general Apostille path for documents from Hague Convention parties, subject to exceptions under Law 4231/2014 and Regulation (EU) 2016/1191; it also describes consular authentication routes for specified reservation and non-party situations. It identifies the Ministry Authentication Department as an authentication authority, says it does not provide translation services, and points users to the certified-translator search route and other Greek-law translation professionals.
