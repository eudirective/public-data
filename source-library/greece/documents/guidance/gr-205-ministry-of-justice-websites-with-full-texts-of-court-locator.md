## Source role and accepted scope

Dated directory and public-access description for official court-decision websites only; not a judgment, legal authority, live service test, or complete case-law corpus.

## Review findings

The notice identifies anonymized Council of State decisions from 1990 onward and a small number of administrative-court decisions as publicly searchable at adjustice.gr under Services > Case-law search, with an approximate corpus of 135,000 at the time of publication. It separately points to the Thessaloniki Administrative Court portal for anonymized .txt decisions and says the full text appears by selecting a decision number. The official Gov.gr locator identifies the Council of State as responsible for the Council of State/Administrative Courts service.
