## Source role and accepted scope

Full dated text of the joint ministerial decision governing registered-minor matching for foster care and adoption; adoption-specific provisions are Articles 9–12, with general provisions in Articles 1–4 and timing in Article 13.

## Review findings

The exact National Printing House API response was HTTP 200 and is identified by the Gazette heading, instrument number, subject, series/issue/date, signature block and colophon. All eight pages were text-extractable with pypdf 6.21.2; no image XObjects or attachments were present. Articles 1–4 define the registered participants, scope, principles and responsible bodies; Articles 5–8 govern foster-care matching; Articles 9–12 govern adoption matching through daily questionnaire-based matching, social-worker/parent meetings, an adjustment period, recorded agreement or rejection, and subsequent application to the competent court. Article 11 sets the adoption adjustment period at a minimum of two weeks and normally a maximum of one month, with at least ten meetings under the stated conditions. Article 13 states that the deadlines provided in the decision begin to run on 20 September 2020; it does not expressly give a general commencement date. Article 12(1) literally refers to Article 7 for the adoption adjustment period, although the adoption-specific adjustment procedure is in Article 11; that textual cross-reference is preserved and its intended legal effect is unresolved.
