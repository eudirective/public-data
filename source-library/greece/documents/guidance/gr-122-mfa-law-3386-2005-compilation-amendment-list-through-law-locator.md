## Source role and accepted scope

Historical wording of Law 3386/2005 Article 77 as reproduced in the government-hosted compilation only.

## Review findings

The 75-page PDF identifies itself as a codification of legislation for entry, residence and social integration of third-country nationals, and lists Law 3386/2005 and amendment laws through Law 3907/2011. Article 77 states a five-day appeal from notification to the Minister of Public Order or an authorized body, decision within three working days, and suspension of expulsion but not detention when detention was ordered.
