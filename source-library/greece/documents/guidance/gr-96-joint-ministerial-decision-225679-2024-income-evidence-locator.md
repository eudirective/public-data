## Source role and accepted scope

Article 2 method and evidence for the family-reunification income condition under Law 5038/2023 Article 84(2)(b).

## Review findings

The official Gazette identifies Decision 225679/2024 and FEK B 5223 dated 17 September 2024. Article 2 states that the sponsor’s personal, stable and regular income is measured by the net annual minimum-wage income calculated over 14 months, with any required family increases under Article 84(2)(b); regularity is evidenced by the sponsor’s social-insurance and tax-compliance records for the preceding year.
