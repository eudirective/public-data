## Review findings

Official EUR-Lex judgment identity and date verified; procedural posture: Preliminary reference from the UK Court of Appeal (Civil Division); Third Chamber. Screened holding: Five-year residence periods completed before 30 April 2006 under earlier EU-law instruments count toward Article 16(1) permanent residence. Mere possession of a valid residence permit under Directive 68/360, without meeting substantive residence conditions, does not by itself make the period legal for that purpose.
