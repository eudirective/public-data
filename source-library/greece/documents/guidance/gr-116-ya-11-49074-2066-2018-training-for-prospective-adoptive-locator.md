## Source role and accepted scope

Original ministerial decision on training programs for prospective adoptive parents and continuing education of adoptive parents, under Law 4538/2018 Article 24.

## Review findings

The 8-page Gazette PDF identifies FEK B 4489/11.10.2018 and the decision number/title. The adoption-specific section sets program aims and subjects, states the candidate program is free, and makes successful completion a prerequisite for entry in the National Register of Prospective Adoptive Parents; Article 9 provides for a completion certificate. Law 4837/2021 Article 30 separately establishes a spouse-child training exception.
