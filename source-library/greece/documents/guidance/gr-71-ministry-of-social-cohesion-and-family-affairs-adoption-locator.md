## Source role and accepted scope

Official Ministry overview of minor-adoption and intercountry-adoption routes and the criteria displayed on that page, as observed on 26 October 2026. This is an administrative content snapshot, not a current eligibility determination or consolidated legal text.

## Review findings

The official Greek page describes adoption as completed by court decision and cites Law 2447/1996, Civil Code Articles 1542–1588, Law 4538/2018, PD 226/1999 and Law 3765/2009 ratifying the 1993 Hague Adoption Convention. It describes an AnyNet route for children in child-protection institutions, direct arrangements after prospective-adopter register entry, and intercountry adoption, stating all culminate in a court decision. It displays adopter-capacity and age criteria, an 18–50-year age difference, a joint-adoption rule for spouses and a civil-union exclusion statement. Its intercountry section describes residence in separate Contracting States, the administrative scope of the Hague process, application through the Central Authority or a recognized service, and identification of the State of origin. These are the Ministry page’s undated statements, not independently verified current requirements or live operating instructions.
