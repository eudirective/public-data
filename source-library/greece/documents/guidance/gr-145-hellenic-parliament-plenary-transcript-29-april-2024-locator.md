## Source role and accepted scope

Official parliamentary legislative record reproducing the text presented and recorded during final plenary consideration of Law 5107/2024 Articles 7 and 9. Article 7 amends Law 4538/2018 Article 26 on adoption/foster-care social investigations; Article 9 updates the National Adoption and Foster Care Council reporting line. Not a promulgated-text or current-consolidation record.

## Review findings

The 119-page Parliament transcript was opened. Pages 15795–15796 reproduce Article 7 and its replacement wording for Law 4538/2018 Article 26; pages 15797–15798 reproduce Article 9 updating the Council's supervising General Secretariat. The final Gazette text at gr-79 was separately reviewed for Articles 7 and 9 and corresponds on the reviewed wording. Article 40 of the final law makes it effective on publication, 1 May 2024.
