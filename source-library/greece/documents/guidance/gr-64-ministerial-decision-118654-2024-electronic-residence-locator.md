## Source role and accepted scope

Original statutory procedure for electronic submission and communication concerning third-country residence-document applications under Law 5038/2023 and PD 106/2007.

## Review findings

The official Gazette identifies Decision 118654/2024. Part A expressly covers electronic filing under Law 5038/2023 and PD 106/2007; Article 1(1) requires initial applications under either instrument to be submitted exclusively through the Ministry's electronic services, subject to stated exceptions. Reopened the official 12-page Gazette PDF on 26 October 2026. Article 3(1) also provides exclusive electronic submission for renewal applications for titles under PD 106/2007; Article 1(3) allows in-person initial filing in sufficiently justified force-majeure cases. The text does not specifically identify Article 17 permanent-residence cards; the scope remains the general PD 106/2007 application framework, not confirmation of an Article17-specific menu or live service.
