## Source role and accepted scope

Original ministerial decision establishing the fostering and adoption information system and special/national registers under Law 4538/2018 Article 27.

## Review findings

The 12-page PDF identifies FEK B 1163/8.4.2019, the decision number/title and the Ministry. Articles 1–2 establish the national system at IDIKA and list the child, prospective-parent, approved-foster and adoption registers and the system purpose. A later 2023 Gazette decision cites this instrument when describing an expansion of the platform.
