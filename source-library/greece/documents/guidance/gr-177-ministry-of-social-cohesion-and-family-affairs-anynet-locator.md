## Source role and accepted scope

Official dated administrative-statistics snapshot for the AnyNet foster-care/adoption information system; accepted for operational context and publication identity only.

## Review findings

The 10-page Ministry PDF is titled as Q3 2026 data from the AnyNet information system. It describes the National Register of Minors, family-reintegration plans, applicant application stages, child age-preference categories, electronic connections and comparative quarterly figures. It says EKKA has institutional responsibility for keeping the system data. The report explains that the cumulative adoption count since electronic matching began can include cases for which a final adoption judgment has not yet issued; applicant preferences may cover more than one child-age category.
