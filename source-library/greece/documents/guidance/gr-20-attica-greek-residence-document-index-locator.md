## Review findings

On 26 October 2026, Greek sections 8.1.1–8.1.2 and 8.3.1–8.3.2 were reviewed. Article 9 section 8.1.1 labels initial issue as five years and no administrative fee; it lists passport, entry-status, insurance, relationship and Union-citizen registration/permanent-residence evidence. Section 8.1.2 labels renewal under Articles 11–12 as five years and no administrative fee and lists passport/card, insurance, Union-citizen permanent-residence and specified continuing-marriage or retention evidence; resources are listed for the stated separation/divorce situations. Both sections separately list four photos, a JPEG2000 CD, EUR 16 standalone-document cost and conditional birthplace evidence. A caution under 8.1.1 refers to a registered-partnership application by a spouse/partner of a Greek citizen, although the heading concerns a Union-citizen family member; its scope on this page is unclear. Article 17 sections 8.3.1–8.3.2 label initial issue and renewal as ten years and no administrative fee; they list passport pages, prior residence-card evidence and specified family-status or death evidence, then separately request photos/JPEG2000 CD, EUR 16 and conditional birthplace evidence. The page does not reconcile its no-administrative-fee headings with the separate card cost. The legacy photo/CD directions and checklist details are not accepted as current.
