## Source role and accepted scope

Historical national implementation text for residence documents issued to beneficiaries under the Withdrawal Agreement.

## Review findings

Article 1 covers UK citizens and family members within Withdrawal Agreement Articles 9 and 10, sets five- or ten-year permit validity, identifies police or Decentralized Administration authority routing, and Article 1(5) sets the initial application window to 30 June 2021. Article 2 lists supporting documents and Article 3 covers fingerprinting.
