## Source role and accepted scope

Incorporation of Directive 2004/38/EC into the EEA Agreement and its express EEA-specific declaration.

## Review findings

The declaration states that Union citizenship has no equivalent in the EEA Agreement, political rights are not supplied by the EEA Agreement, and residence rights for third-country family members are limited to the derived rights specified there.
