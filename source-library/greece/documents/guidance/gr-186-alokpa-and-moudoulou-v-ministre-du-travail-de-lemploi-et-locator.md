## Review findings

Official EUR-Lex judgment identity and date verified; procedural posture: Preliminary reference from the Luxembourg Administrative Court; Second Chamber. Screened holding: Articles 20 and 21 TFEU do not preclude refusing residence to a third-country sole carer of minor Union citizens born and resident in Luxembourg without Luxembourg nationality or prior free movement, where the children fail Directive 2004/38 conditions or are not deprived of the substance of Union citizenship. The referring court must determine the facts, including whether refusal would force the children to leave the Union.
