## Source role and accepted scope

The narrow amendment to Article 1(2) of Decision 118654/2024 for initial issuance of Law 5038/2023 residence permits of types A1, A2, A3 and A7: applications within the Ministry’s competence must be submitted in person at its one-stop service, subject to that service’s scheduling.

## Review findings

Article 1 inserts a sentence requiring the specified initial humanitarian-category permit applications (A1, A2, A3 and A7) to be lodged exclusively in person at the Ministry’s one-stop service. Article 2 makes the decision effective on publication. The Gazette copy identifies FEK B 5007 and 22 September 2025; the National Printing House API locator is recorded for rediscovery.
