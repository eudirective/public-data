## Source role and accepted scope

National operational description of residence permits for beneficiaries under Article 18(4) of the EU–UK Withdrawal Agreement, including Greek transition from the earlier to new security print format.

## Review findings

The Police page states that Article 18(4) permits use the uniform third-country residence-permit format under Regulation (EC) No 1030/2002 as amended by Regulations 380/2008 and 2017/1954, and cites Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/1945. It says Greece changed security-document printing on 10 December 2024; labels the new format as applying from that date; preserves validity until expiry for earlier WA documents; and identifies permanent-residence status, family-member status, full labour-market access and fingerprinting.
