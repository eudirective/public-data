## Source role and accepted scope

Region of Crete public service for requesting consultation support on intercountry adoption. Covers the regional inquiry-submission route only; it is not the formal adoption application or a national end-to-end procedure.

## Review findings

The Region of Crete page identifies an online citizen request using Taxisnet, submission of a consultation-request form and identity-card copy, routing to the responsible directorate, protocol-number issuance and officer completeness review. It states availability through the Region’s Social Welfare Directorate across Crete and lists 18 attachments (17 country PDFs and one Word form). The page cites the adoption statutes and 2018 Ministry circular. The linked Word form was not readable by the connected page reader; sampled Ethiopia and Romania PDFs have no current publication date and were not accepted as current country checklists.
