## Review findings

Official Gov.gr search content says the birth is declared at the maternity hospital where delivery occurred. It lists joint declaration by both parents, specified exceptions for declaration by the father, and declaration by the mother for a single-parent family. It lists identification-document categories, contact details and a Greek-bank IBAN; after filing it describes automatic birth-registration-certificate issuance, AMKA assignment, family-register entry, insurance registration and creation of a birth-allowance application, with SMS notification. The TCN document list asks for a valid passport or recognized travel document and a visa or residence permit, then refers to an international-protection applicant card “for those who have been granted asylum”; that wording is internally imprecise and is not treated as a legal rule.
