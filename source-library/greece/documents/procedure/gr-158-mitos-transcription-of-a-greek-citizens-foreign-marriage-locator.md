## Source role and accepted scope

Dated MITOS procedure description for transcribing a Greek citizen’s foreign marriage record at the Athens Special Registry in the circumstances described by the page and the cited exception rules.

## Review findings

MITOS describes mandatory transcription of foreign marriage records concerning Greek citizens registered in valid municipal registers. It states that since 1 January 2022 foreign civil-status events concerning Greek citizens are normally registered by the competent Greek consulates, subject to KYA 45927/2021 exceptions. The Special Registry route is shown as in-person and non-digital, free, with an estimated 30-minute processing time. The page describes an authenticated and officially translated original foreign marriage certificate, or conditionally a final Greek court judgment with service/finality evidence; it also displays identity, authorization and residence conditions. The service page was last updated 14 October 2026 and cites Law 344/1976, Law 4674/2020, KYA 45927/2021 and other related material.
