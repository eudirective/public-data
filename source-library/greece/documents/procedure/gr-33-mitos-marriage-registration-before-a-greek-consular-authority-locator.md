## Source role and accepted scope

Registration by a Greek consular authority of a marriage abroad by Greek citizens.

## Review findings

The official Greek-language MITOS procedure concerns Greek citizens marrying abroad, civilly or religiously, who declare the marriage in person to the territorially competent Greek consulate. It requires at least one spouse to be registered in a Greek municipal register. Displayed material includes the local civil marriage act or relevant Metropolis certificate, identity evidence, and recent municipal-register certificates for both spouses retrieved ex officio. The page displays a €10 application fee and €10 per copy, with late-declaration fines under PD 16/2014. Its steps include checking documents, retrieving registry records, preparing/checking/signing the act, payment and issuance; the act is automatically transmitted to the competent Municipality. The page lists Law 5089/2024 and Regulation (EU) 2016/1191 among related legal materials.
