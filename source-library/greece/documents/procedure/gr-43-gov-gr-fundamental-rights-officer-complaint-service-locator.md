## Source role and accepted scope

Dated description and locator for complaints to the Ministry Fundamental Rights Officer concerning access to territory, reception, and international-protection procedures.

## Review findings

The service page says non-EU citizens may submit a signed complaint about alleged fundamental-rights violations by a public authority. It describes preliminary assessment and referrals for admissible complaints.
