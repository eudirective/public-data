## Source role and accepted scope

Selected second-instance asylum procedure under Law 5307/2026, including conditional adjournment when legal assistance is not provided.

## Review findings

The page summarizes appeal procedure, hearing, adjournment, annulment and right-to-remain provisions. It states that failure to provide free legal assistance under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 can support adjournment only where irreparable or difficult-to-repair harm and a reasonable prospect of success are found.
