## Review findings

Official Gov.gr search content displays the Ministry of the Interior as competent and creating/maintaining authority. It says personal Taxisnet credentials are required; for a marriage performed in Greece and registered at the relevant registry office, the most recent extract is immediately available online if issued on or after 8 May 2013, while earlier records and previous marriages go through the Municipality or Special Registry Office for later issuance. For marriages performed abroad and registered at the Athens Special Registry Office, it states that the most recent extract is immediately available for records drawn up since 22 January 2018 and that other requests go through the Municipality/Special Registry Office. The page says the response is placed in the Gov.gr inbox with Gov.gr security features and offers myKEPlive as an alternative route. The English indexed excerpt also contains the incongruous phrase “complete documents of births” in the marriage section; this is preserved as a page-text anomaly and not interpreted as a marriage-certificate category.
