## Source role and accepted scope

Post-judgment registration of a minor adoption in a Greek civil registry. Not an adoption merits procedure or a foreign-judgment recognition rule.

## Review findings

The current MITOS record describes a no-fee, non-digital application to the civil registry at the adoptive parent’s place of residence. It identifies two documents: a final court adoption decision and a certificate that the decision was served on the competent prosecutor. It reports one-day estimated service time and lists hierarchical appeal and silent rejection for the registration process.
