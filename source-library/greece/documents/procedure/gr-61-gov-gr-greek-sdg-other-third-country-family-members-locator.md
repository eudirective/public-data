## Review findings

Indexed official Greek Gov.gr content describes other-family-member grounds of material dependence, prior household membership in the country of origin, serious health grounds requiring personal care, and the converse case where the EU citizen depends on the applicant. For durable partners it lists the Union citizen's declaration and evidence such as shared housing, a common child or adoption, or other long-term legal, social or economic ties; insurance evidence is also mentioned.
