## Source role and accepted scope

Displayed procedure for UK citizens and family members falling within Withdrawal Agreement Articles 9 and 10, for a residence document under Article 18(4).

## Review findings

MITOS identifies Hellenic Police authorities, no digital filing, in-person or postal filing, 4–9 documents, a 16-euro fee, estimated completion of 15 days, five- or ten-year validity, and a hierarchical appeal within 20 days with a decision within 30 days. Its legal references include Law 4652/2020 and KYA 4000/1/113-α΄.
