## Source role and accepted scope

General administrative process for obtaining a certificate concerning recognition of a foreign court judgment; not family-specific.

## Review findings

MITOS reports that a lawyer files an application in voluntary jurisdiction seeking a Greek decision recognizing the foreign judgment, after which the interested person requests the recognition certificate. It identifies a translated and apostilled foreign judgment and cites PD 503/1985 (Code of Civil Procedure), Articles 323 onward.
