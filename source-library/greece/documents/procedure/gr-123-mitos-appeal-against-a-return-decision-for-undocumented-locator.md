## Source role and accepted scope

Dated operational description for third-country nationals without travel documents who entered Greece irregularly and have a return-and-detention decision; the service entry describes an in-person administrative appeal.

## Review findings

The MITOS page states a five-day appeal period; filing by the applicant or legal representative in person at an Aliens Directorate or Police Directorate; examination by the Directorate head within five days; no digital service, no fee, estimated service time of 7–45 days, and a help obligation for legal and language assistance. It describes approval as lifting the return-and-detention decision and later deletion from EKANA; it describes continued detention after rejection. The page identifies the five-day Article 77 route and links Law 3386/2005 Article 77 and Law 3907/2011 Article 28.
