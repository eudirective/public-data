## Source role and accepted scope

Dated description for third-country nationals without travel documents who entered Greece irregularly and received a return-and-detention decision. This is not the EU-family route.

## Currentness conflict

MITOS cites Law 3907/2011 Article 28, repealed by Law 5226/2025 Article 40 effective 8 September 2025. It states a five-day appeal-review period, while Law 5226/2025 Article 14(1) states three working days for police-issued return decisions. The successor route and operational procedure are unresolved. The later MITOS modification date does not establish current legal accuracy. The excerpt is retained as a dated page snapshot only.

## Use limits

- Do not apply the stated five-day filing period to EU citizens, family members, residence-title decisions, or other return routes.
- The page states both that no supporting documents are required and that an identity document is needed; verify the operative requirement with the responsible authority.
- No submission or runtime check was performed; current practice is unverified.
- Greek is controlling. Check the live page and operative successor legislation before reliance.
