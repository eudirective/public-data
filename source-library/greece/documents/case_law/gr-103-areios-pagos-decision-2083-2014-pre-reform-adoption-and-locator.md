## Source role and accepted scope

Historical intestate succession involving a minor adopted before Law 2447/1996 and natural relatives who died after that law took effect.

## Review findings

The Court held that, absent a later full-integration order under Article 57(3), the adoptee retained inheritance rights against natural relatives. It annulled Larissa Court of Appeal 530/2011 and remanded.
