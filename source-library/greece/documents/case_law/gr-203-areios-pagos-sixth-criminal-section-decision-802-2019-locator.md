## Source role and accepted scope

The Sixth Criminal Section’s review of cassation from a Thessaloniki appellate conviction for repeated facilitation of unlawful third-country national residence and participation in a criminal group, including paid fictitious marriages organized to obtain residence documents.

## Review findings

The official Areios Pagos decision page identifies the Sixth Criminal Section, the cassation proceeding and the operative order. It discusses PD 106/2007 Article 26 (abuse of rights) and Law 4251/2014 Article 29(6), and holds in the recorded criminal case that organizing a marriage solely to obtain a residence right can constitute facilitation of unlawful residence. The Court rejected the cassation application and additional grounds, leaving the conviction in place.
