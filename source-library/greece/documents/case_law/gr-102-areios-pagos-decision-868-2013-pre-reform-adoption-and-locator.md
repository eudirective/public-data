## Source role and accepted scope

Historical inheritance-certificate dispute involving a pre-reform adoption and deaths of natural parents on 26 December 1996 and 27 February 1997.

## Review findings

The Court held that the first-instance court had misapplied the adoption and transitional inheritance rules when it rejected the adoptee’s application for inheritance certificates concerning her natural parents. It annulled the refusal and remanded for further adjudication.
