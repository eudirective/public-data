## Source role and accepted scope

Cassation concerning Greek recognition of a German family-court judgment establishing paternity, after heirs challenged an earlier Greek recognition decision.

## Review findings

The official indexed Greek text identifies the D Civil Section, the procedural history and the disputed public-policy ground. The Supreme Court held that the appellate court misapplied the rules governing recognition of foreign judgments by treating the difference between German law and the Greek paternity limitation rule as sufficient, without applying the required case-specific international-public-policy analysis. It accepted the relevant cassation grounds, annulled Athens Single-Member Court of Appeal Decision 2736/2021 and remitted the case to that court with a different judge. The Court did not itself finally grant or refuse recognition of the German paternity judgment. The indexed text supplies the hearing, decision and pronouncement dates.
