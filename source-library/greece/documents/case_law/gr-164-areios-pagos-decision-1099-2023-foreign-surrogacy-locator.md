## Source role and accepted scope

Full official judgment text on an application to recognize a South African parentage/surrogacy order and foreign birth record, including the cassation issue and remand disposition.

## Review findings

The official decision page identifies AP 1099/2023 and the proceedings. The applicants sought recognition and Greek registry recording of a South African order establishing parentage following a surrogacy arrangement. The Court found that the appellate court had treated the foreign order as an adoption despite the pleaded surrogacy and parentage facts, annulled the appellate decision and remanded for further proceedings. It did not finally decide recognition of the foreign order.
