## Source role and accepted scope

Historical inheritance effects of a minor adoption made before Law 2447/1996, including the statutory full-integration route in Article 57(3).

## Review findings

The Court held that the Article 57(3) full-integration order is the relevant event creating reciprocal intestate inheritance rights with the adoptive family; without it, the corresponding adoptive-family inheritance right did not arise. It annulled Athens Court of Appeal 4906/2010 and remanded.
