## Source role and accepted scope

Historical inheritance effects of a minor adoption made under pre-30 December 1996 law, where no full-integration order under Law 2447/1996 Article 57(3) was recorded.

## Review findings

The Court held that the adoptee retained intestate inheritance rights against her natural father, who died in 2006. It annulled Athens Court of Appeal 3118/2009 and remanded for rehearing by a differently constituted bench.
