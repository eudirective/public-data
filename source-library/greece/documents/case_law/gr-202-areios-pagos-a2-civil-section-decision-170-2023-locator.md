## Source role and accepted scope

Cassation by heirs challenging Greek recognition of an Austrian judgment establishing paternity in a matter involving an Austrian daughter and her deceased Greek father.

## Review findings

The official indexed Greek text identifies the A2 Civil Section, the Austrian paternity proceedings and the Greek recognition challenge. The Court rejected the heirs’ cassation arguments, including their public-policy and abuse-of-right objections. It held, on the facts, that the limitation period under Greek law and the daughter’s delay did not by themselves make exercise of the Austrian-law right abusive or the Austrian judgment’s effect contrary to Greek public policy. The operative order rejects the cassation application against Thessaloniki Single-Member Court of Appeal Decision 2496/2018, leaves the recognition result in place, directs forfeiture of the cassation fee and sets costs. The indexed text supplies hearing, decision and pronouncement dates.
