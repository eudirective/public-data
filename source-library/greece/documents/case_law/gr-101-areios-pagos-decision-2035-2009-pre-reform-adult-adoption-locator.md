## Source role and accepted scope

Historical intestate succession in an estate following a pre-reform adult adoption.

## Review findings

The dispute concerned an adult adopted in June 1996 by his natural father’s second wife and the adoptive mother’s succession after his intestate death in January 2003. The Court rejected natural cousins’ cassation challenge, leaving the appellate disposition recognizing the adoptive mother’s inheritance position in that estate.
