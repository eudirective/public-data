## Source role and accepted scope

The final cassation decision in the same litigation as Areios Pagos Plenary Decision 17/2008, concerning recognition in Greece of the 1984 Michigan adult-adoption judgment.

## Review findings

The official indexed Greek decision text identifies AP 1956/2009, recounts the Plenary ruling and reviews the remaining cassation grounds. The A2 Civil Section rejected the remaining application for cassation, leaving the refusal to recognize the Michigan adoption in place, and ordered costs. The indexed text supplies hearing, decision and publication dates.
