## Source role and accepted scope

The Fifth Criminal Section sitting as Council reviewed an appeal against execution of a Czech European Arrest Warrant and addressed whether a Czech EU citizen had a sufficient residence connection to Greece for the purpose of domestic execution of sentence.

## Review findings

The official Areios Pagos page identifies the Fifth Criminal Section sitting as Council and the EAW appeal. In the residence evidence discussion, the Court states that the appellant’s PD 106/2007 Article 8 EU-citizen registration certificate did not, by itself, establish permanent residence at the named address because duration had not been verified and no Article 16 permanent-residence document had issued. The Court rejected the appeal and upheld execution of the EAW.
