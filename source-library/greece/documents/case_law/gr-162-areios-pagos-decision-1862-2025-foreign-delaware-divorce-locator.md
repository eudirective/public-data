## Review findings

The official decision page identifies AP 1862/2025 and the procedural history. The Supreme Court reviewed recognition of a Delaware divorce under CPC Article 905(4), discussed the Article 323 conditions and whether a Greek judgment in the same divorce matter barred recognition, and dismissed cassation. It records that the lower court treated the earlier-filed Delaware case as not displaced by later Greek proceedings and recognized the Delaware divorce judgment. The decision also discusses the attempted Brussels IIa argument and notes the third-country context.
