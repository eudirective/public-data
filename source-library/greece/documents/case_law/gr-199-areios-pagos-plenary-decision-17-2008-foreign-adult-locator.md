## Source role and accepted scope

The Plenary’s review of the referred fifth cassation ground in litigation over recognition in Greece of a 1984 Michigan adult-adoption judgment involving an adoptive father who was a bishop and had taken monastic vows.

## Review findings

The official indexed Greek decision text identifies a Plenary civil decision and its procedural history. The majority rejected the referred fifth cassation ground, accepting the lower court’s public-policy basis for refusing recognition of the Michigan adoption; it then remanded the remaining cassation grounds to the A2 Civil Section. The page also records an eight-judge dissent disputing the public-policy analysis. The indexed text gives the hearing, decision and public-pronouncement dates.
