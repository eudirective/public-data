## Source role and accepted scope

Article 92 replaces Law 4538/2018 Article 26(1) on social-service investigations; the provision refers among other provisions to Civil Code Article 1557, relevant to adoption social investigation. Article 92 preserves the cross-reference to the periods in Articles 10 and 11 for the reassigned worker’s report.

## Review findings

The official Gazette PDF identifies Law 4611/2019 and FEK A 73/17.05.2019. Article 92 expressly replaces Article 26(1) of Law 4538/2018 and the replacement text covers the relevant social-service investigation and reassignment rule. The replacement text preserves Civil Code Article 1557 within the listed investigations and directs a reassigned social worker to complete the report within the period provided in Law 4538/2018 Articles 10 and 11. Those original provisions concern foster-care cases; the legal meaning of that cross-reference for adoption investigations remains open.
