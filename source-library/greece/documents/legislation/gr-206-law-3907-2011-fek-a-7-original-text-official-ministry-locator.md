## Source role and accepted scope

Articles 28(1)–(5) (return-decision remedies and legal assistance) and 30(1)–(6) (detention grounds, reasons, objections, review and duration).

## Review findings

The PDF title page identifies the Official Gazette of the Hellenic Republic, Series A, issue 7, 26 January 2011, and Law 3907/2011. Article 28 provides differentiated administrative remedies for police return decisions and return decisions embedded in residence-title decisions, administrative reconsideration and temporary suspension, assistance/legal-aid provisions, and an annulment action. Article 30 sets original return-detention grounds and safeguards, written reasons, an objection route before the administrative court president/designated judge, periodic review and duration limits. The law’s Chapter C identifies itself as adapting Greek law to Directive 2008/115/EC. These are original-text observations only.
