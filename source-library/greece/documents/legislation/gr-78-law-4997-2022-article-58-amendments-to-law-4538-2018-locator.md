## Source role and accepted scope

Article 58 targeted amendments to Law 4538/2018 Articles 5, 14, 16, 24 and 26, including adoptive-parent training provisions in Article 24.

## Review findings

The official Ministry-hosted 56-page Gazette reproduction identifies Law 4997/2022 and FEK A 219/25.11.2022. Article 58 was opened; it modifies the specified Law 4538/2018 provisions, including Article 24 on training programmes for prospective adoptive parents.
