## Source role and accepted scope

Articles 42–45 (targeted legal-aid amendments) and Article 57 (replacement of Law 3068/2002 Article 15 and consequential amendment of Law 4636/2019 Article 108(1)).

## Review findings

The official Gazette issue identifies Law 4689/2020, FEK A 103/27 May 2020. Articles 42–45 amend legal-aid application timing, lawyer appointment, recovery of expenses, and related criminal legal-aid provisions. Article 57 replaces Law 3068/2002 Article 15 in full. In the replacement, paragraph 3 concerns territorial venue for the specified aliens-law disputes, paragraph 5 provides appeal to the Council of State, and paragraph 9 concerns nationality acquisition/loss. The exact Article 57 text was reviewed from the official National Printing House PDF (PDF pages 21–22; printed pages 1835–1836).
