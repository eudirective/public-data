## Source role and accepted scope

Article 1, section sixth, insofar as it replaces Code of Civil Procedure Article 800 paragraphs 1, 2, 4 and 5: specified Greek-nationality jurisdiction, consent declarations, third-party opposition, and participation/appeal capacity of adoptees aged at least 16.

## Review findings

The Gazette identifies Law 4335/2015 and Article 1. Section sixth expressly lists Article 800 paragraphs 1, 2, 4 and 5 among the replaced provisions and supplies replacement text. The reviewed provisions establish Greek-court jurisdiction where the adopter or adoptee is Greek and the specified capital-court rule; private, non-public consent declarations and related hearing arrangements; a six-month-from-knowledge and three-year-from-finality third-party opposition period with an information route for a natural parent who did not consent; and the personal appearance and independent appeal capacity of adoptees aged 16 or older.
