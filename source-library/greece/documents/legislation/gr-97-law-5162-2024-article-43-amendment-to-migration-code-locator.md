## Source role and accepted scope

Article 43 amendment of Law 5038/2023 Article 95(2), limited to family members of specified investment-residence holders and its express derogation from Article 84(1).

## Review findings

Article 43 replaces the reference to Migration Code Article 100 with Article 100A and reproduces Article 95(2): holders of permits under Articles 96–100A may be accompanied by the listed family members, whose permits end with the sponsor’s permit, by derogation from the two-year condition in Article 84(1).
