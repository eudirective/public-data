## Source role and accepted scope

Bounded historical text and amendment notes for Civil Code adoption Articles 1542–1546 and Article 1557. Articles 1542 and 1557 are the versions identified in the compilation as established on 30 December 1996 under Law 2447/1996 Article 1; Articles 1544–1546 include selected 1997/1999 amendment notes. Not accepted as current consolidated law.

## Review findings

The official Ministry of Justice Codes index links a 630-page PDF credited to the Athens Bar Association's Isokratis legal-information database. Direct text review found the Article 1542 entry on PDF page 466 and the Article 1557 entry on page 472. Both entries state 30.12.1996 and attribute their wording to Law 2447/1996 Article 1, FEK A 278. Article 1542 contains the historical minor-adoption rule and best-interests statement; Article 1557 describes a pre-adoption social investigation and court report. Articles 1543–1546 were already reviewed: Article 1544 has a note for Law 2721/1999 Article 28; Article 1545 has a note for Law 2521/1997 Article 19(2); Article 1546 states the spouse-consent rule. The upload path is 2019/10, but the database compilation cutoff is unstated, so these are bounded historical source-text findings only.
