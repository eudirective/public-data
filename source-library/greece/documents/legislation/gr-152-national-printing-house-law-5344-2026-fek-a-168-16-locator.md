## Review findings

The official Gazette issue masthead identifies FEK A 168/16.10.2026, cross-checked against the Hellenic Parliament docket. The embedded Greek text was extracted and reviewed for Articles 178–183, 187 and 217. Articles 178–180 and 182–183 amend the listed CPC provisions in connection with mediation/court mediation; Article 181 amends the stated retrial ground concerning prohibited State aid. Article 187 adds Law 3226/2004 Article 1(2A), extending legal aid for specified civil-claim mediation participation and costs. Article 217 makes Articles 178–180, 182–183 and 186–187 effective 1 March 2027, and Article 181 effective 1 January 2027.
