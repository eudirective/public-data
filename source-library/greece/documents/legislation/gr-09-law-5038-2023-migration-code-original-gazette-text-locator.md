## Source role and accepted scope

Original enacted text for the third-country-sponsor family-reunification route, especially Articles 83–90; procedural guarantees at Article 16; delegated-document provisions at Article 176. This is distinct from family members of Union citizens governed by PD 106/2007 and from family members of Greek citizens.

## Review findings

The Gazette identifies Law 5038/2023 and FEK A 81. Article 83 identifies scope and the Directive 2003/86/EC route; Articles 84–85 set conditions and application/examination; Article 87 covers permit duration; Article 88 grounds refusal, withdrawal or non-renewal; Article 16 provides a two-month administrative reconsideration route and judicial annulment review for residence-permit decisions; Article 176 delegates category-specific and general long-stay visa documentation. Article 83(3) excludes EU-citizen family members from Articles 84–90.
