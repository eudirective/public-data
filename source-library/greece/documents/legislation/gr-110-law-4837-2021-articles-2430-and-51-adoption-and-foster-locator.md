## Source role and accepted scope

Articles 24–30 amend Law 4538/2018 provisions. Articles 24–29 primarily address foster-care governance, registers, eligibility, placement, supervision and foster-parent training; Article 30 amends Article 24(3) to exempt prospective adoptive parents applying to adopt a spouse’s child from training and the certificate requirement. Article 51 amends Civil Code Article 1579 on adult adoption.

## Review findings

The official Gazette PDF identifies Law 4837/2021 and FEK A 178/01.10.2021. Articles 24–30 were reviewed at their headings and targeted provisions, including the express spouse-child adoption training exception in Article 30. Article 51 (page 20) replaces Civil Code Article 1579: adult adoption is permitted only if the adoptee is related to the adopter up to the fourth degree by blood or affinity, or the adopter was the adoptee’s foster parent.
