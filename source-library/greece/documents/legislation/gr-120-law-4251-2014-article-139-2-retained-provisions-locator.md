## Source role and accepted scope

Article 139(2) only: continued force of specified Law 3386/2005 provisions, including Articles 76–78, and PD 106/2007 after repeal of the remaining 3386/2005 provisions.

## Review findings

The official National Printing House Gazette PDF identifies Law 4251/2014 and FEK A 80, dated 1 April 2014. Article 139(1) repeals Law 3386/2005 subject to stated exceptions; Article 139(2) expressly retains Articles 76, 77, 78, 80, 81, 82, 83 and 89(1)–(3), as well as PD 106/2007. This verifies that PD 106/2007 Article 24(1)'s reference to Law 3386/2005 Article 77 was not simply rendered obsolete by the 2014 Migration Code.
