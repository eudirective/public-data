## Source role and accepted scope

Article 62 only, the replacement text for Law 4652/2020 Article 10 on UK Withdrawal Agreement rights and residence-document implementation.

## Review findings

Article 62 applies Part Two of the Withdrawal Agreement, particularly Article 18, to residence and related rights; it authorizes a joint ministerial decision to identify issuing authorities, implementation details and residence-document categories.
