## Source role and accepted scope

The original act promoting foster care and adoption; adoption chapter, Articles 20–25, as originally enacted. Article 22 also supplies the replacement text for PD 226/1999 Article 1 as at 16 May 2018. Article 26(1) also includes social investigations under Civil Code Article 1557 and sets a reassignment/report framework.

## Review findings

The official Ministry-hosted 16-page Gazette reproduction identifies Law 4538/2018 and FEK A 85/16.05.2018. The adoption chapter begins at Article 20. Article 22 expressly replaces Article 1 of PD 226/1999; paragraphs 1–2 identify the then-recognized domestic and intercountry adoption services. This is the replacement text at the 2018 enactment date, not proof of present wording or operation. Article 26(1) includes investigations under Civil Code Article 1557; where the competent service cannot investigate, it has a reasoned 15-day notice duty and a reassigned social worker is directed to complete the report within periods in Articles 10 and 11. In the original act, Article 10 concerns contract-based foster placements and Article 11 concerns court-ordered foster placements, so the fit of that cross-reference to an adoption investigation under Article 1557 does not itself establish an adoption-specific worker-assignment deadline.
