## Source role and accepted scope

Law 4674/2020 Article 28 replaces Law 344/1976 Article 13 with registry-record correction routes; Articles 30–31 cover Greek citizens' foreign civil-status declarations and the Special Registry exception framework.

## Review findings

The official 60-page Gazette issue identifies Law 4674/2020 and FEK A 53/11 March 2020. Article 28 replaces Law 344/1976 Article 13: a final court decision is generally required; specified non-time/place/date/year/hour errors may be corrected with Prosecutor/Justice-of-the-Peace approval after inquiry; obvious clerical mistakes may be corrected by the registrar after inquiry. Article 30 requires Greek citizens abroad to declare foreign civil-status events before competent Greek consular authorities without displacing local declaration; Article 31 replaces Law 344/1976 Article 43 with a Special Registry exception/infeasibility framework.
