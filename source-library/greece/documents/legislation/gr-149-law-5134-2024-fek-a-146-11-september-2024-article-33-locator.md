## Source role and accepted scope

Article 33, replacing Code of Civil Procedure Article 740 on first-instance competence and appeals in voluntary-jurisdiction matters.

## Review findings

Article 33 replaces Article 740. Paragraph 1 assigns Article 739 cases to the single-member First Instance Court and its judge where provided, except assisted-reproduction cases assigned to multi-member courts. Paragraph 2 prohibits prorogation of competence. Paragraphs 3–4 allocate specified appeals to multi-member courts and other appeals from single-member courts to single-member courts of appeal, subject to the text’s conditions.
