## Source role and accepted scope

Law 5226/2025 Articles 13–16, 28, 40(1) and 41(1): decision form/notice, remedies and judicial protection, return safeguards, detention, the preserved PD 106/2007 route, repeal of Law 3907/2011 Chapters C–F, and commencement.

## Review findings

The official Gazette PDF identifies Law 5226/2025, FEK A 154, 8 September 2025. Article 28(1) excludes people who continue to enjoy free movement under the Schengen Borders Code and PD 106/2007 unless they no longer meet its conditions. Article 28(2) makes PD 106/2007 Articles 22–24 the return-procedure basis and applies compatible provisions of Law 5226 supplementarily on authorities, deadlines, service, guarantees and judicial protection, subject to more favourable PD 106 provisions; Article 28(3) addresses specified Greek-citizen family members. Articles 13–15 respectively cover decision form/reasons/notice/translation, administrative and judicial remedies and interim protection, and safeguards during voluntary departure or postponed removal. Article 14 distinguishes police-issued return decisions (five-day administrative appeal, three-working-day decision period, suspensive effect of return but not detention) from returns integrated into residence-title refusal/revocation (ten-day application for reconsideration, five-working-day decision period); it also identifies temporary administrative suspension, provisional judicial protection and annulment proceedings. On 26 October 2026, Article 16 was additionally reviewed as the successor statutory detention provision: it specifies necessity and alternatives, detention grounds, written reasons and authority, judicial objections, notice and immediate release if unlawful. Article 40(1) repeals Chapters C–F of Law 3907/2011 from commencement, including its Articles 28 and 30; Article 41(1) makes the law effective on publication, 8 September 2025. Article 28(2) separately preserves the PD 106/2007 Articles 22–24 route for persons within the stated EU free-movement scope, subject to compatible supplementary provisions and more favourable PD rules. The Article 28(2) preservation clause supplies a limited partial source mapping to Directive 2004/38 Article 37 for the covered EU-family return procedure; it does not inventory more favourable rules in other areas.
