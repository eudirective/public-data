## Source role and accepted scope

Articles 23–24: Article 23 adds Law 4538/2018 Article 9(3) on connection of minors and prospective foster parents. Article 24 reissues Law 2447/1996 Article 7, including paragraphs 3–4 on the social-report deadline for a specific-child adoption application and paragraph 5 on connection of minors with registered prospective adoptive parents and delegated implementation.

## Review findings

The official Gazette PDF identifies Law 4704/2020 and FEK A 133/14.07.2020. Article 23 adds Law 4538/2018 Article 9(3). Article 24 reissues Law 2447/1996 Article 7: paragraphs 3–4 provide an exclusive six-month period for the social-service report concerning an application for a specific child, measured from submission or service of that application, irrespective of requested supporting material, with a possible specially reasoned three-month extension; the six-month period is limited to domestic habitual-residence cases, while intercountry cases may receive up to two exceptional extensions. Paragraph 5 requires registration in the National Register of Prospective Adoptive Parents before delivery and delegates the matching procedure. The specific-child court-report clock is not the same trigger as AnyNet’s separately described pre-matching worker-assignment clock.
