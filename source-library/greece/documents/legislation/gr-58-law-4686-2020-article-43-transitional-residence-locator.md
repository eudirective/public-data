## Source role and accepted scope

Article 43 transitional residence-document rules for specified EU-family and Greek-family cases under the former KYA 23443/2011, including references to PD 106/2007.

## Review findings

Article 43(1) addresses specified holders of older KYA-issued permits and possible permanent-residence documents under PD 106/2007 Article 13; Article 43(2) addresses certain personal residence rights under PD 106/2007 Articles 11 and 12.
