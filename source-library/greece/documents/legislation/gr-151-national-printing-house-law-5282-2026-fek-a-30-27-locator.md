## Source role and accepted scope

Final official Gazette text of Law 5282/2026 Articles 13–27 amending selected Code of Civil Procedure provisions, and Article 76 commencement as it applies to those articles.

## Review findings

The official Gazette masthead identifies Law 5282/2026, FEK A 30/27 February 2026, cross-checked against the Hellenic Parliament voted record/docket. Articles 13–27 amend CPC Articles 96(3), 119, 147(2), 215(1), 237, 238, 468, 469, 495, 509(2), 520(2), 523(2), 524(1), 527, 547(2), 548 and 568. Article 76(1) makes the law effective on publication except Article 76(2), which delays only Articles 36, 40 and 41 to 1 May 2026; the reviewed Articles 13–27 therefore took effect 27 February 2026.
