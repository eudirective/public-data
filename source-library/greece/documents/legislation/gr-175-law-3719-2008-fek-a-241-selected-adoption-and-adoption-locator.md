## Review findings

The official Ministry-hosted four-page Gazette reproduction identifies FEK A 241 of 26 November 2008 and Law 3719. Articles 16–18 replace Civil Code Articles 1552 (judicial substitution of consent), 1553 (hearing relatives in specified cases) and 1565 (adoptive-child name). Article 20 adds an adoption-jurisdiction rule to CPC Article 800(1) where the adopter or adoptee is Greek, with venue in the capital. Article 21 replaces Law 2447/1996 Article 7(2), requiring notice to the competent social service for direct arrangements before surrender or within one month. Article 22 changes territorial social-service allocations under PD 226/1999 and the intercountry information process; those named service allocations are historical.
