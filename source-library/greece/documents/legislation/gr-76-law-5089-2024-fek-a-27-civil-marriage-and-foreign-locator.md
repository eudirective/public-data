## Source role and accepted scope

Article 3 amending Civil Code Article 1350(1) to define marriage as between two persons of different or same sex; Articles 10–11 concerning specified same-sex marriages and parentage relationships created abroad, including certain foreign adoptions, and their recognition in Greece.

## Review findings

The official 8-page Gazette PDF identifies Law 5089/2024 and FEK A 27/16.02.2024. Article 3 (page 1) amends Civil Code Article 1350(1) to state that marriage is concluded between two persons of different or same sex. Articles 10–11 were also opened and reviewed. Article 11 provides a targeted rule for parentage recorded in third-country public documents or judgments and for specified third-country adoptions; where based on a judgment it refers recognition to the applicable rules for recognition of judgments. Article 15 states that the law enters into force upon publication unless a provision states otherwise.
