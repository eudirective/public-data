## Source role and accepted scope

Targeted provisions: Articles 102–103, 135, 141, 154–157, 219, 261–262 and 265. Articles 154–157 are accepted only for the separate asylum border-return/detention route and its statutory scope and authority; Article 158 crisis derogations and Article 159 return hubs are not included.

## Review findings

Articles 102–103 and 219 establish free asylum legal guidance, Appeals Authority legal assistance/representation, and court legal aid by analogy to Law 3226/2004. Article 262 repeals Law 4939/2022 from commencement; Article 261 contains transition rules. On 26 October 2026, the official 122-page Ministry-hosted Gazette reproduction was reviewed at Articles 154–157 (PDF pages 85–86; printed pages 4210–4211) and Article 265 (PDF page 120; printed page 4245). Article 154(1)–(3) states that the Part implements Regulation (EU) 2024/1349, sets its scope, and excludes persons who enjoy EU free movement within the stated Schengen Borders Code and PD 106/2007 scope. Article 155 identifies the Hellenic Police as execution/removal authority and the National Return Coordinator as coordinator. Article 156 keeps covered rejected asylum-border applicants under non-entry status; links the return decision to Regulation (EU) 2024/1348 Article 37; applies Regulation 2024/1349 Article 4 and selected Law 5226/2025 provisions; directs cases not completed within the Article 4(2) period into Law 5226 Part B; and refers non-compliance sanctions to Law 5226 Article 27. Article 157 provides individualized written detention orders, grounds and facilities, includes the border detention period in the Law 5226 Article 16(5)–(6) maximum after transition to the general route, and applies Law 5226 Articles 16–18 analogically to unregulated matters. Article 265 makes 12 June 2026 the general commencement date; Articles 154–157 are not among the listed publication-date exceptions. This is a separate statutory asylum border-return route, not an amendment to Law 5226 Articles 14 or 16 and not the preserved PD 106/2007 free-movement-family route. ## Use limits

- Article 102 concerns free guidance during the administrative procedure on written request no later than registration; Article 219 concerns guidance in the responsible-Member-State determination procedure.
- Article 103(2) concerns free legal assistance and representation before the Appeals Authority on written request no later than 48 hours before the Article 135 appeal deadline; court proceedings refer to Law 3226/2004 applied analogously.
- Article 261 preserves the prior regime for specified pre-12 June 2026 cases and provides a temporary guidance arrangement no later than 30 September 2026; cohort selection and post-transition delivery need operational review.
- The law repeals Law 4939/2022 subject to express transition rules; old MITOS citations do not establish current law.
- No complete amendment or implementation audit for Law 3226/2004 is established.
- Greek is the controlling language.
