## Source role and accepted scope

Article 117(2)–(4): adoption court venue, manner of declaring consent, and commencement date.

## Review findings

Article 117(2) adds that adoption is conducted by any single-member First Instance Court in Greece. Article 117(3) replaces the first sentence of Article 800(2), requiring consent declarations before the court conducting the adoption in a separate office without publicity. Article 117(4) states that the provisions in paragraphs 1–3 take effect on 16 January 2017.
