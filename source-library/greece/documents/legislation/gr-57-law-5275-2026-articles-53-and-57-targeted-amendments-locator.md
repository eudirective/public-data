## Source role and accepted scope

Law 5275/2026 Articles 53 and 57 within the inspected scope. Article 53 reproduces the replacement text of PD 106/2007 Article 3 (paragraphs 1–3) and its transitional rule for pending family-card issuance/renewal applications; Article 57 amends the specified Law 5226/2025 provisions.

## Review findings

The official National Printing House 64-page PDF identifies Law 5275/2026, FEK A 17, published 6 February 2026. The complete relevant Article 53 text was reviewed. It replaces the introduction to PD 106/2007 Article 3(2) and sets out Article 3 as amended: Article 3(2) now says a five-year Residence Card under Article 10 is issued to (a) other family members outside Article 2(2) who meet the listed dependence, household, serious-health/personal-care, or support-of-the-Union-citizen criteria, and (b) partners in a duly evidenced stable relationship. Article 53(2) directs that pending issuance or renewal applications filed before commencement be examined under the amended text. Article 69 makes the law effective on publication unless otherwise stated. The replacement wording also removes the original Article 3(1) phrase excluding Greek nationals; the legal effect of that wording change is not determined here. Article 57 was reviewed for its targeted amendments to Law 5226/2025 Articles 4, 22(4) and 23(4), not Articles 13–16 or 28.
