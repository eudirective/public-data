## Reviewed scope

Official three-page Greek-English form addressed to Hellenic Police SIRENE for access to national SIS data. It requests identity evidence and includes optional context fields for visa refusal, residence-permit refusal/renewal and denied entry.

## Disposition and limits

Accepted as the official 2021 form snapshot only. It cites former SIS II Regulation 1987/2006 and old system terminology. The current replacement framework, recipient address, route and response notice remain unverified. No form was submitted and no text is reproduced locally.

Accepted by link only. No local source text is retained. No automated retrieval rule is accepted.
