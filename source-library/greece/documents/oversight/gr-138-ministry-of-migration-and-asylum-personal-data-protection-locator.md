## Source role and accepted scope

Dated general privacy policy for Ministry-owned, developed or supported facilities, digital environments and applications.

## Review findings

The policy describes Ministry processing for users of its services and applications, lists data-subject rights including access and correction, gives the Ministry DPO contact, describes response within one month with possible extension by two months, and states that when the Ministry acts as processor it forwards requests to the controller. Section 14 points to the Hellenic DPA complaint route.
