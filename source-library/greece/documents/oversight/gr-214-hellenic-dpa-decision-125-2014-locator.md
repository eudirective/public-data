## Reviewed scope

The decision concerns a third-country national's request to erase SIS II and the Greek National List of Undesirable Aliens records after a family-reunification application supported by a family-status certificate that the authorities treated as forged. The DPA discusses the legal basis for a SIS II alert and continued retention under Regulation 1987/2006 and the national framework then in force. The DPA dismissed the individual complaint.

## Disposition and limits

Accepted as an official, historical DPA oversight decision by link only. It is a fact-specific third-country family-reunification matter, not a decision about free movement under Directive 2004/38/EC or residence cards for EU citizens' family members. It predates GDPR and the current SIS Regulations. Do not use its outcome, statutory references, retention period, or procedure as a statement of current law. Current data-subject routes are separately represented by gr-208 and current primary sources.
