## Review findings

The Greek page and English parallel identify Regulations (EU) 2018/1861 and 2018/1862, describe access, rectification and erasure rights, and route requests to the Greek S.I.Re.N.E. Bureau of the Hellenic Police by email or post and through Greek consular authorities. The page asks for identity and contact details, requires authorization or power of attorney for a third-party request, says the issuing Member State is responsible for creating/updating/deleting alerts, and lists judicial or supervisory-authority remedies. The page also references Law 4624/2019 but includes former Law 2472/1997 among the legal citations.
