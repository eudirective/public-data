# Swiss Foreign Nationals and Integration Act (FNIA / AIG / LEI)

**Reference ID:** CH-04  
**Official publisher:** Swiss Confederation, Federal Chancellery (Fedlex)  
**Fedlex record:** https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2007/758/en  
**Systematic number:** SR 142.20  
**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026  
**Record type:** Maintained official legal-reference note; not a retained statute copy

## Scope

The Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration (FNIA; German AIG; French LEI) regulates foreign nationals' entry, exit, residence, family reunification and integration in Switzerland. It is a central federal statute but does not apply in isolation. Its scope is qualified by federal legislation and international treaties. In particular, EU/EFTA free-movement rights and Schengen rules can alter which provisions govern. The Act also interacts with the Asylum Act and implementing ordinances, including the Ordinance on Admission, Residence and Employment (OASA/VZAE).

The outcome depends on the person's nationality and status, the sponsor's status, the requested procedure and the applicable treaty or statutory route. This note is an orientation to the official legal record, not a complete explanation of any immigration entitlement, deadline or remedy.

## Navigation and legal dependencies

Use the Fedlex record to select the current or historical text for the relevant date. Check the Official Compilation amendments and commencement provisions when a recent amendment or transition may matter. Then identify the implementing ordinance and any treaty framework that applies. For family reunification, distinguish at least the FNIA route for third-country nationals from the AFMP/EFTA route, and check the Asylum Act where the sponsor has asylum or provisional-admission status.

The Act includes provisions on admission and integration, entry and exit, residence and employment, family reunification, termination of residence and procedural matters. The maintained note does not substitute for checking the operative article, implementing provision, canton practice or appeal notice.
