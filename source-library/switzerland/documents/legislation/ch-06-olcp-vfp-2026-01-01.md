# Swiss Ordinance on the Free Movement of Persons (OLCP / VFP)

**Reference ID:** CH-06  
**Official publisher:** Swiss Confederation, Federal Chancellery (Fedlex)  
**Official record:** https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/261/fr?version=20260101  
**Systematic number:** SR 142.203  
**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026  
**Record type:** Maintained official legal-reference note; not a retained ordinance copy

## Scope and function

The Ordinance on the Free Movement of Persons (OLCP in French; VFP in German) implements the Swiss free-movement framework under the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (AFMP) and the EFTA Convention. Its provisions address personal scope, residence and related procedures, including qualifying family members and certain service providers. It must be read with the relevant treaty, the FNIA and other implementing rules.

The ordinance is not the treaty itself and does not make all EU/EFTA or third-country family situations equivalent. Its personal scope, exceptions and transitional rules matter. For each case, establish the person's nationality, sponsor and treaty status, and consult the applicable agreement and annexes before relying on an OLCP provision.

## Current record and version

Fedlex identifies the instrument as Ordonnance sur la libre circulation des personnes (OLCP), systematic number 142.203, adopted 22 May 2002. The official Fedlex record for the 1 January 2026 version is available at the language-specific ELI locator linked above. The official indexed text describes the ordinance as implementing the AFMP and EFTA Convention and sets out the personal scope, including EU/EFTA nationals, qualifying family members, certain posted workers and specified UK acquired-rights beneficiaries.

The 2026 version route is explicit in the Fedlex URL (version=20260101). It is a historical/versioned locator for that date, not a promise that no later amendment will apply after 1 January 2026. Fedlex's general page is JavaScript-based; use the official text and amendment list for the date relevant to the matter.

## Interpretation and cross-checks

Read OLCP together with the AFMP, its Annex I and applicable Joint Committee decisions; for EFTA nationals, also check the EFTA Convention and relevant annexes. Confirm the interaction with FNIA and the Ordinance on Admission, Residence and Employment (OASA/VZAE), including explicit exclusions or special routes. The SEM's OLCP directives are administrative guidance and should be checked as a separate source.

This note is a source guide, not a complete account of treaty rights, family categories, transitional provisions or individual eligibility. Do not infer a right from the ordinance's title or apply an EU directive to Switzerland without confirming the Swiss treaty framework.
