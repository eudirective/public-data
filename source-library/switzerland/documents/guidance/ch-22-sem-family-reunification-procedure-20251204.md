## Accepted scope

This operational directive concerns specified family-reunification procedures for third-country nationals under the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration (AIG) and the Asylum Act. It addresses Swiss foreign representations, cantonal migration authorities, and SEM. The PDF opened on 29 October 2026 contains 18 pages and identifies itself as Directive 322.3-12, dated 4 December 2025.

## Procedure and authority boundaries

For ordinary family reunification under AIG Articles 42 ff., a Swiss representation usually conducts preliminary review and forwards the file to the competent canton; the canton examines and decides, with SEM approval reserved in specified cases. The directive separately assigns processes for provisionally admitted persons and recognized refugees. It describes long-stay visa D steps and document checks, including voluntary checks that require the applicant's written consent and must be proportionate.

The SEM parent page lists the directive as Annex to section 6.1.1 and separately lists Annex I, a country list. The country list has not been independently reviewed here. The source note does not map all cantonal procedures, appointments, fees, remedies, or document requirements.

## Exclusions and maintenance limits

- The directive expressly excludes family reunification under the EU-Switzerland Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (AFMP). Keep AFMP/EU-EFTA routes separate.
- This is administrative guidance, not the statutory basis. Read it with the current AIG, VZAE, OEV, and Asylum Act.
- Use the official parent index to resolve the current directive and separate annex. Do not infer currentness from a saved copy of this note.
