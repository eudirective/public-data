## Factsheet scope

The factsheet describes family reunification under the Agreement on the Free Movement of Persons (AFMP) for EU/EFTA nationals who have a Swiss right of stay. It states, in summary:

- Eligible family can include a spouse and children under 21 or supported; supported parents may also qualify, except for students. The sheet notes special rules for Liechtenstein nationals.
- Suitable accommodation is required. Employees’ eligibility is not conditioned on their financial situation; self-employed and economically inactive sponsors must have sufficient means. Students and trainees are limited to spouses and dependent children.
- The family member’s stay is derived from the EU/EFTA sponsor’s AFMP right and ordinarily has the same permit duration.
- Listed entry/residence evidence includes identity documents, a visa where required (with a stated Schengen residence-permit exemption), proof of family relationship, and support/co-residence evidence for supported relatives.
- Spouses and eligible children may work throughout Switzerland, including self-employment; ascending-line relatives may not work.
- It addresses equal treatment, the consequences of divorce or death, and refusal/revocation in specified fraud, document-falsification, sham-marriage, or illegal-adoption cases.

The live FAQ also lists spouses, children or grandchildren under 21 or financially dependent, and dependent parents or grandparents; for students it limits family reunification to spouse and supported children. The FAQ states that admitted spouses and children may work irrespective of nationality. Differences in the FAQ’s listed relatives and the PDF’s wording must be resolved against the current AFMP, OLCP/VFP, applicable SEM directives, and competent cantonal authority.

## Scope limits

This is a dated operational factsheet and a maintained summary, not binding legislation, a verbatim source copy, or a complete statement of AFMP rights. It does not establish that every listed requirement or exception remains current in every case, nor does it decide nationality-specific visa rules, canton procedure, or individual entitlement. The FAQ is dynamic and broader than family reunification; check its live date and linked materials again before use. Keep AFMP/EU-EFTA routes distinct from Swiss-citizen and ordinary third-country routes under the Foreign Nationals and Integration Act.
