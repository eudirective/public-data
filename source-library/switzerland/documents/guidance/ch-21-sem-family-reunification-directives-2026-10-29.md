# Swiss SEM Directives on Family Reunification under the FNIA

**Publisher:** Swiss State Secretariat for Migration (SEM)

**Record type:** Maintained official guidance index note; linked PDF copies are not reproduced

## What this source is

This SEM page is the official index for Chapter 6, family reunification, within the SEM's guidance for the foreign nationals area. It links to the current SEM Directives and Commentary on the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration (FNIA/LEI), related attachments, and a dated chronology of revisions.

The page is a navigation point for administrative guidance. The directives explain SEM and cantonal practice but do not replace the FNIA, the Asylum Act, the Ordinance on Admission, Residence and Employment, the Free Movement Agreement or applicable case law. The correct framework depends on the sponsor's and family member's legal status.

## Version state checked

The French family-reunification page displays:

- the general LEI directives, excluding Chapter 4, at **15 June 2026**;
- the separate Chapter 4 directives on residence with gainful employment at **30 June 2026**;
- an annex under item 6.1.1, a directive on family-reunification procedure for third-country nationals dated **4 December 2025**, with a country list on the same date.

The SEM chronology says the general LEI directive update of 15 June 2026 entered into force on that date. The specific third-country procedure directive states that it enters into force on **1 January 2026** and replaces the 25 June 2012 instruction on DNA profiles and civil-status-document examination. The page itself displays a last-change date of 12 December 2025, while the linked general directives have newer 2026 editions; rely on each linked file's own edition date, not the parent-page modification date.

The current family-reunification materials cover status-specific pathways. The third-country procedure directive describes coordination among SEM units, Swiss representations abroad and cantonal migration authorities. Do not infer that its process applies unchanged to beneficiaries of free-movement rights, Swiss sponsors, refugees or persons with provisional admission.

## How to use and maintain the source

1. Start from the official index and open the relevant chapter and annex for the person's status.
2. Record the edition date of the exact PDF used; the principal directive set has separate publication dates for Chapter 4 and the rest.
3. Follow the chronology for later changes and inspect the linked PDF itself for the operative passages.
4. Check the underlying federal act and ordinance, treaty framework and applicable canton or mission procedure before applying a rule to a case.
