## Official source

- [FDFA: Information regarding entry into Switzerland and residence](https://www.eda.admin.ch/en/information-regarding-entry-into-switzerland-and-residence)

## Verified content

The official page is published 1 February 2026. It distinguishes EU/EFTA citizens from other nationals and identifies the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) as the responsible federal authority for entry and residence information. It routes readers to SEM for visa requirements, nationality-based identity/visa rules, entry information, the short-stay calculator, free movement, long stays, and permit types; it also links to cantonal migration and labour-market authorities.

For non-Swiss nationals outside the EU/EFTA, the page says entry conditions depend on nationality, purpose, and length of stay and directs visa applicants to the Swiss representation competent for their place of residence. The page includes an ETIAS section but says no action is then required and the EU would announce the start date. Because those statements and linked services can change, check the current official SEM and EU pages before use.

## Scope limits and maintenance

This is a cross-authority navigation hub, not an eligibility rule or a substitute for SEM, legislation, treaty text, cantonal procedures, or mission instructions. It does not determine an individual visa requirement or residence right. Some page wording refers to “EU-25/EFTA”; do not rely on that passage for present-day registration deadlines or nationality coverage without checking current SEM rules.

On review, check the FDFA publication date and links, then verify the relevant SEM page, nationality list, calculator, canton, or mission directly. Keep AFMP/EU-EFTA cases distinct from ordinary third-country routes and short-stay Schengen entry.
