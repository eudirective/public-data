## What was verified

The official English page identifies itself as SEM section “VII. Visa,” says the documents are not available in English and directs readers to German, French or Italian. It links to the nationality-based identity and visa provisions (Annex CH-1, List 1) and an “Entry provisions” page. It displays **Last modification 1 October 2012**.

The German counterpart, opened on 29 October 2026, displays the same 1 October 2012 modification date and links to separate short-stay Schengen and stays over 90 days under Swiss rules, plus Annex CH-1 and entry FAQs. The language pages therefore establish an official navigation route, but their old modification date means this landing page cannot establish that its content or linked documents are current.

## Accepted use and limits

Use this page only as a SEM navigation lead to visa materials. For a current issue, open the applicable German, French or Italian instruction and attachment directly and verify its date and legal status. The current Swiss Visa Handbook edition was not established in this review. Do not substitute an EU Visa Code consolidation for Swiss instructions.

This record does not reproduce the handbook, Annex CH-1, or other linked documents; it does not decide visa eligibility, residence authorization, or family-member facilitation. Check the traveller’s nationality, purpose, duration, residence status and applicable AFMP/family rules separately.
