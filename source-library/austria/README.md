# Austria national-source candidates

This is the first country register in the EEA-plus-Switzerland expansion. It currently contains 27 official discovery candidates and a sustainability assessment, not an active retrieval pack. The register now covers NAG registration and permanent-residence certificates under §§ 53 and 53a, plus Swiss eligibility under § 57, the Information Freedom Act, general remedies, sample administrative-court decisions, and central BMEIA visa and residence guidance. No source is accepted, and no Austria action is exposed by the monitor MCP yet. Rule verdicts remain undetermined because retrieval, validation, conversion, and replay are not implemented.
