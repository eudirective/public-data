## Summary

There are no implemented Austria source rules to rate. The candidate manifest contains 34 discovery candidates, all unaccepted. Official publisher documentation and sample pages support several plausible starting routes, but that is design evidence only. Retrieval feasibility from the plugin runtime, response validation, conversion, offline replay, and change detection remain unimplemented or unverified; no sustainable-rule conclusion follows from the candidate URLs.

RIS is the strongest prospective legislation source. Its official [Bundesrecht handbook](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/RisInfo/HandbuchBundesnormen.pdf) documents current-section links built from the statute number and paragraph and explains the version metadata exposed with each section. The current NAG § 53 page was also opened and shows the statute/section identity, effective date, latest-update date, document number, and ELI. The current NAG-DV § 5 page maps the residence-card and permanent-residence-card forms to Annex E/F; current Annex 5/6 pages identify separate PDF attachments with document numbers and ELIs, but the payload bytes remain uncaptured. Current IFG § 15 separately confirms the DSB’s advice/training and accompanying evaluation role; it is not a judicial remedy. NAG §§ 53 and 53a add the registration-certificate and permanent-residence-certificate stages; § 57 expressly extends the §§ 51–56 framework to qualifying Swiss free-movement cases. RIS OGD requires no special registration, but the interface terms rule out unscheduled, concurrent, or bulk polling. The official RIS FAQ disclaims completeness and continuous availability and identifies the authentic RIS application as authoritative. A monitor should therefore produce review candidates and preserve the last accepted text on errors; it should not present cached text as newly verified.

The research reader's extracted view of the current § 53 page presents each paragraph in a standard form and again with expanded number words (for example, “§” and “Paragraph”). This may reflect alternate accessible/presentation nodes or reader extraction behavior; the raw publisher HTML was not captured in this review. It is a concrete conversion risk: do not deduplicate by matching legal sentences or promote converted output until an exact RIS response is captured, the operative text node is identified, and an offline replay proves that headings, numbering, footnotes, and full text are preserved.

The central BMEIA pages are useful national operational sources, but they do not replace mission-specific consular instructions. The general visa page contains volatile fees, deadlines, prior-consultation information, family-member exemptions, and review guidance. The existing EU guidance pack already registers Annex 16 through the Commission's title-matched catalogue resolver; Austria should reference that cross-cutting EU record instead of copying its download URL into an Austrian pack.

## Repair order and closure conditions

1. Build an Austria candidate pack using stable RIS current-section routes. Store the request URL and final URL, raw response bytes and SHA-256, content type, retrieval time, RIS statute/section/document identifiers, ELI, language, and current version/effective-date evidence. Reject mismatched identities, access-denied pages, incomplete bodies, unexpected language/format, and contradictory versions.
2. Prefer the public RIS current-section pages for a bounded registered set only after capturing and inspecting the raw response. The official RIS FAQ recommends manual current-law downloads for small selections when daily currentness is unnecessary; it separately constrains larger OGD API queries to serial requests no faster than one every two seconds, outside Austrian office hours or on weekends/Austrian holidays, with attribution and no mass downloads. Do not imply those API conditions are terms for the website route without checking. Classify outages and access denials separately; preserve accepted content and retry later.
3. Capture RIS fixtures for consolidated sections, including duplicate or parallel text, tables, headings, amendment/version history, and annex links. Implement a fail-closed body selector and deterministic conversion. Replay offline against captured official responses before assigning a sustainability verdict.
4. For RIS case law, retain individual court, case number, ECLI, document number, decision date, and the exact official record bytes. Treat these as immutable decision candidates; separately define query terms, courts, date windows, and result pagination for discovery. A query with no results is not proof of absence or complete coverage.
5. For BMEIA and oesterreich.gv.at, pin first-party canonical routes and page headings; capture only identified main content, visible dates when present, and separately identified official links/forms. Use authoritative topic directories for route recovery. Report material text, link, fee, date, and attachment changes for review; do not auto-promote.
6. Close mission-specific scope by naming applicant locations or by explicitly limiting the register to central Austrian information. Search each relevant BMEIA mission page separately for appointments, representation, checklists, and family-member intake.
7. Complete the open national searches in `source-list.md` before describing Austria's coverage as broad. Add court decisions and the NAG decision-specific appeal route separately from general delay remedies.
