# Austria source-rule sustainability assessment

Assessment date: 18 October 2026  
Scope: all 25 Austria discovery candidates currently recorded in `candidate-manifest.json`. This is a proposed-rule review; Austria does not yet have an implemented source-pack action, and no retrieval, conversion, or acceptance rule is active.

## Summary

No Austria source rule can yet be rated sustainable because there is no Austria adapter or runtime action to inspect. The expanded evidence supports feasible official discovery routes for legislation, practical guidance, information access, delay remedies, and consular instructions. The full acquisition chain remains **undetermined** until capture, source-identity checks, conversion, offline replay, and change detection are implemented and inspected. No candidate is accepted.

RIS is the strongest prospective legislation source. Its current-section locator combines the statute number with a paragraph and exposes document identifiers, ELI, effective dates, and version history. NAG §§ 53 and 53a add the registration-certificate and permanent-residence-certificate stages; § 57 expressly extends the §§ 51–56 framework to qualifying Swiss free-movement cases. RIS OGD requires no special registration, but the interface terms rule out unscheduled, concurrent, or bulk polling. The official RIS FAQ disclaims completeness and continuous availability and identifies the authentic RIS application as authoritative. A monitor should therefore produce review candidates and preserve the last accepted text on errors; it should not present cached text as newly verified.

The central BMEIA pages are useful national operational sources, but they do not replace mission-specific consular instructions. The general visa page contains volatile fees, deadlines, prior-consultation information, family-member exemptions, and review guidance. The existing EU guidance pack already registers Annex 16 through the Commission's title-matched catalogue resolver; Austria should reference that cross-cutting EU record instead of copying its download URL into an Austrian pack.

## Repair order and closure conditions

1. Build an Austria candidate pack using stable RIS current-section routes. Store the request URL and final URL, raw response bytes and SHA-256, content type, retrieval time, RIS statute/section/document identifiers, ELI, language, and current version/effective-date evidence. Reject mismatched identities, access-denied pages, incomplete bodies, unexpected language/format, and contradictory versions.
2. Prefer the official current-section HTML for a bounded registered set. Use OGD only if needed for discovery or monitoring and follow the current FAQ: no registration, serial requests spaced at least two seconds apart, outside Austrian office hours or on weekends/Austrian holidays, attribution, and no mass downloads. Classify outages and access denials separately; preserve accepted content and retry later.
3. Capture RIS fixtures for consolidated sections, including duplicate or parallel text, tables, headings, amendment/version history, and annex links. Implement a fail-closed body selector and deterministic conversion. Replay offline against captured official responses before revising any verdict above undetermined.
4. For RIS case law, retain individual court, case number, ECLI, document number, decision date, and the exact official record bytes. Treat these as immutable decision candidates; separately define query terms, courts, date windows, and result pagination for discovery. A query with no results is not proof of absence or complete coverage.
5. For BMEIA and oesterreich.gv.at, pin first-party canonical routes and page headings; capture only identified main content, visible dates when present, and separately identified official links/forms. Use authoritative topic directories for route recovery. Report material text, link, fee, date, and attachment changes for review; do not auto-promote.
6. Close mission-specific scope by naming applicant locations or by explicitly limiting the register to central Austrian information. Search each relevant BMEIA mission page separately for appointments, representation, checklists, and family-member intake.
7. Complete the open national searches in `source-list.md` before describing Austria's coverage as broad. Add court decisions and the NAG decision-specific appeal route separately from general delay remedies.

## Evidence boundary

Official RIS legislation, RIS Judikatur, BMEIA, oesterreich.gv.at, Datenschutzbehörde, Volksanwaltschaft, and N-Lex pages/results were inspected on 18 October 2026. They establish current official discovery routes and reveal material constraints; they do not demonstrate that the plugin can fetch the pages, parse complete bodies, replay conversions, detect future changes, or cover every Austrian authority. The GitHub source-library mirror failed synchronization on an unrelated archived handbook blob and was not used as evidence for Austria rule behavior.

A real-browser check for RIS § 17 was attempted, but the Playwright wrapper could not start because npm registry DNS resolution failed (`EAI_AGAIN`). The web reader also denied or timed out on some RIS pages, including the direct § 8 routes noted above. These are reader/environment observations; plugin-runtime retrieval remains unverified.
