# AIMA information for EU citizens and their family members

**Publisher:** Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA), Portugal

## What the hub covers

AIMA’s “Nacionais UE e Familiares” hub points to information for EU citizens and their family members, including EEA, Swiss and Andorran nationals and family members. Its reviewed page says that the free-movement and residence right derives directly from EU Treaties and refers to Lei n.º 37/2006. It distinguishes the underlying right from the documents used to establish or formalize a person’s status.

The English URL currently returns a Portuguese-language page in the indexed official content. Use the Portuguese service pages as the operative route and check the individual page before relying on a form, fee, appointment channel or document checklist.

## Main service routes described by AIMA

- **EU citizen staying over three months:** apply for a Certificado de Registo at the Câmara Municipal for the place of residence. AIMA’s route page states a 30-day filing period after the first three months in Portugal. Its stated supporting material includes valid identification and a declaration relevant to the person’s worker, self-sufficient or student status.
- **Non-EU family member staying over three months:** request a Cartão de Residência through an AIMA service appointment. AIMA’s page directs the person to register interest through its contact form, selecting the EU residence-card subject. It lists identity and relationship evidence and additional evidence for dependent family members, where applicable.
- **Permanent residence after five years:** AIMA’s current pages route permanent EU certificates and permanent family-member cards through the Portal de Renovações. The pages state that the portal became the submission route from 1 July 2026; some requests and appointments already made through the prior channel are handled under the transition described on those pages.

These are summaries of the routes displayed during review. AIMA’s pages are operational guidance and can change independently of the legislation.

## Legal cross-check and limits

The Diário da República consolidated record for Lei n.º 37/2006 reports the law in force and a last amendment date of 2 June 2023. The law sets the underlying residence framework, including municipal registration after three months for EU citizens, residence conditions, family-member rights, and permanent residence after five years. The AIMA page should be read with that law and any later applicable legislation.

The reviewed AIMA card page includes operational document requirements that should be checked against the applicant’s exact family relationship and circumstances. Its examples do not replace the statutory definitions, the right to prove status by legally admissible means, or an authority’s individual assessment. Do not infer that every listed document applies to every family member.
