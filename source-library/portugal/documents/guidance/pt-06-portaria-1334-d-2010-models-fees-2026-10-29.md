# Portaria n.º 1334-D/2010: EU residence-document models and fees

## Instrument and version

Portaria n.º 1334-D/2010 approves the document models and fees associated with Portugal's EU free-movement residence regime. The Diário da República (DRE) consolidated record displayed “Em vigor,” a last alteration date of 31 January 2024, and a selectable consolidated view dated 24 August 2026 when reviewed.

The record shows amendments by Portaria n.º 164/2017, Portaria n.º 225/2020 and Portaria n.º 13/2024. Portaria n.º 32/2024 corrected the commencement arrangement for Portaria n.º 13/2024: the latter's fee, allocation and service provisions apply to procedures begun from its entry into force, 30 days after publication. The current consolidated record incorporates that correction. DRE warns that its consolidation has no legal value and does not replace consultation of the underlying acts.

## What the consolidated instrument covers

The Portaria approves models for:

- EU-citizen registration certificates (Anexo I);
- EU-citizen permanent-residence certificates (Anexo II);
- residence cards for third-country family members of EU citizens (Anexo III); and
- permanent residence cards for those family members (Anexo IV).

The 2024 amendment replaced the EU registration-certificate and EU permanent-certificate models in Annexes I and II. The family-member card models in Annexes III and IV have their own earlier amendment history, including Portaria n.º 164/2017 and Portaria n.º 225/2020. The DRE page displays those annexes and change histories; its text extraction does not render every model image as usable text. Consult the official annex or original publication when the visual form or security feature matters.

## Fee schedule shown in Article 3

The consolidated Article 3 states the following issuance fees:

| Application channel and age | Fee |
| --- | ---: |
| Digital, over 25 | €15 |
| In person, over 25 | €18 |
| In person, under 25 | €15 |
| Digital, under 25 | €12.50 |
| In person, child up to 1 year | Exempt |

It also states:

- **Urgent issuance:** €53 for persons over 25 and €50 for persons under 25 for the Article 2 documents, with hand delivery on the same working day.
- **Address change without replacing the document:** €3.
- **First issuance to a minor under 6:** Article 5 says the applicable fee is reduced by 50%. Consider its interaction with the age/channel schedule and the specific procedure rather than assuming an across-the-board amount.
- **External service for Article 2 documents:** €40, in addition to issuance or replacement charges.
- **Secure postal delivery for Article 2 documents:** €15, in addition to issuance or replacement charges.
- **EU registration certificate:** Article 4 allows a municipality to set a municipal component of the service charge, capped at 50% of the Article 3 amount. The municipality’s actual amount must be checked locally; the national schedule alone does not state the final amount for every municipality.

The Article 2 urgent, external-service and postal provisions concern the permanent-residence certificate and family-member residence-card documents covered there. They should not be transposed to every EU residence procedure.

## Responsibilities and related law

The Portaria allocates EU registration-certificate personalization and issuance between municipalities and AIMA. AIMA handles applications for EU permanent-residence certificates and third-country family-member residence cards; INCM is responsible for producing and personalizing those documents under Article 6.

The governing residence rights, eligibility and application deadlines are in Lei n.º 37/2006, not created by this model-and-fee Portaria. See the [current DRE consolidated Lei n.º 37/2006 record](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/2006-128792849) and the maintained [AIMA service-route note](pt-05-aima-eu-citizens-family-hub-2026-10-29.md). Confirm any later amendments and applicable procedure before relying on a fee or form.
