## Accepted scope

This record covers Book I, Chapter III of the Portuguese Civil Code, “Direitos dos estrangeiros e conflitos de leis” (rights of foreigners and conflict of laws), beginning at Article 14. It is a source locator for Portuguese conflict-of-laws rules relevant to personal status, family relations, foreign law and connected civil matters. It is not a transcription of the whole Code or a complete analysis of every provision in that chapter.

The official consolidated record and article pages provide the operative text. Articles especially likely to require review for the source-library's family/civil-status work include:

- **Articles 14–24:** civil-rights treatment of foreigners, conflict-rule qualification, reference to foreign law, renvoi, multi-system states, evasion of law, international public policy, ascertainment of foreign law, and acts aboard vessels or aircraft.
- **Articles 25–33:** scope and determination of personal law, including nationality and rules for stateless persons and persons lacking capacity.
- **Articles 49–61:** conflict rules for capacity and form of marriage, spouses and marital property, parent-child relations, adoption and related consent requirements.
- **Articles 46–48:** property rights, capacity concerning immovables, and intellectual property, where those subjects are relevant.
