# Vreemdelingencirculaire 2000: maintained source and amendment note

- Source ID: `nl-13`
- Instrument: Vreemdelingencirculaire 2000 (Vc), BWBR0012288
- Responsible authority: Minister of Asylum and Migration; maintained/published by the Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND)
- Official consolidated record: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0012288/
- Official publication series: Staatscourant; individual WBV decisions amend the circular
- Checked: 29 October 2026

## Nature and use

The Vc is the Dutch immigration policy circular. This note is a maintained official-source index and dated amendment checkpoint. It is not the consolidated policy text and does not reproduce the circular. The Vc contains policy rules and interpretation; distinguish it from the Vreemdelingenwet, Vreemdelingenbesluit and Voorschrift Vreemdelingen. Check the applicable statutory and EU-law provisions independently.

## Latest amendment confirmed in this review

The latest located 2026 amending decision is WBV 2026/21, signed 30 September 2026, published as Staatscourant 2026, 32951 on 2 October 2026, and effective 1 October 2026. The official publication replaces or revises numerous sections and the Vc abbreviations list. Its stated subject matter includes updates to identification and screening procedure, return/non-refoulement policy, medical evidence requirements, residence policy and asylum-related policy. The publication also records alignment changes following CJEU judgment C-202/25 (Tadmur). Consult the publication itself to identify the exact provision text relevant to a case.

Other 2026 amendment notices located during this review include WBV 2026/20 (Staatscourant 2026, 31113, published 10 September), WBV 2026/19 (26802, 10 August), WBV 2026/18 (26388, 7 August), WBV 2026/17 (24782, 10 July), WBV 2026/16 (8653, 9 July), WBV 2026/15 (23550, 7 July), WBV 2026/14 (20943, 11 June), WBV 2026/11 (18956, 5 June), WBV 2026/10 (19013, 3 June), WBV 2026/9 (18219, 2 June), WBV 2026/8 (17856, 21 May), WBV 2026/7 (16278, 4 May), WBV 2026/5 (14512, 22 April), and earlier notices. This is a set of located notices, not a certified exhaustive amendment register.

## Currentness limits

The Wetten.nl consolidated record could not be accessed by the research client during this review. Accordingly, the consolidated edition date, all changes after WBV 2026/21, complete policy text, and current status of every provision are not independently confirmed here. Before applying any policy paragraph, inspect the live official consolidated record, its effective-date display and amendment history, plus the relevant primary legislation and binding EU law. An amendment's publication or effective date does not by itself establish the current wording of an unexamined paragraph.

## Maintenance

At each review, check BWBR0012288 on Wetten.nl and search the official Staatscourant for subsequent WBV decisions. Record the last confirmed decision, signature/publication/effective dates, affected sections, and any gap between the official consolidated display and the latest gazette amendments. Keep this note as an index and checkpoint; do not represent it as complete policy text.
