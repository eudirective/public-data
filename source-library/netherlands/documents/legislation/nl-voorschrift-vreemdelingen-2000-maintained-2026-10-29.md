- Checked: 29 October 2026

## Confirmed scope

The VV 2000 is a ministerial immigration implementing rule made under the Vreemdelingenwet 2000 and Vreemdelingenbesluit 2000. The official gazette record identifies the 205th amendment, dated 5 October and published 14 October 2026. Article II makes it effective the day after publication, 15 October 2026. Article I replaces Annex 1, the airport list associated with Article 2.1(1)(a), concerning carriers' obligation to make copies of passengers' travel documents on covered routes. The official explanatory note says the list is updated based on experience data and identifies Damascus, Tirana and correction/order changes in this amendment.

This is a maintained source reference and amendment checkpoint, not a reproduction or complete article-by-article digest of the consolidated VV 2000. The official Wetten.nl page was not retrievable in the research client during this review; therefore its displayed consolidated edition date, all other amendments incorporated as at 29 October, complete text, annexes and current status were not independently verified. Do not infer that Staatscourant 2026, 35717 is the only post-April amendment. Consult the live consolidated record and its amendment history before relying on a provision or annex.

## Maintenance

At each review, inspect the Wetten.nl record for the displayed version/effective interval and linked amendment history, then compare later Staatscourant amendments against the relevant articles and annexes. Retain each dated observation and keep this note explicitly scoped if the consolidated text cannot be checked. This document does not establish topic coverage or replace the official text.
