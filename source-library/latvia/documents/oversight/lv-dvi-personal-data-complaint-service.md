Description:

In response to a complaint concerning the processing of personal data pursuant to Article 78(2) of the General Data Protection Regulation, the National Data Inspectorate shall provide no later than 3 months.

Provide natural persons (data subjects) with the possibility to lodge a complaint regarding the alleged illegal processing of personal data.

Process description

Related documents

If a person considers that an unlawful processing of personal data has taken place under her or her aegis, custody or under the auspices of him or her, the person shall complete a complaint regarding the processing of personal data (https://www.dvi.gov.lv/lv/iesniegumu-samples). The complaint shall contain details of:

• the applicant;

• a person (institution/organisation/merchant/natural person or other person) who allegedly unlawfully processed the personal data of the applicant;

• the relationship with the person who allegedly unlawfully processed the applicant's personal data;

• the nature of the unlawful processing of personal data by the applicant;

• whether, in connection with allegedly illegally processing of the applicant's personal data, the applicant has targeted a person who allegedly unlawfully processed the applicant's personal data, etc.

The submission shall be accompanied by a copy of documents relating to or indicating the possible illegal processing of the personal data of the applicant.

A completed complaint regarding the processing of personal data shall be submitted to the State Data Inspectorate
