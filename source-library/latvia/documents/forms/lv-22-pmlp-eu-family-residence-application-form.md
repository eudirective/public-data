## Form purpose and sections

The eight-page form is bilingual (Latvian and English), asks the applicant to use block capitals, and is used to request a document certifying residence rights for a Union citizen or a Union citizen’s family member. The current PDF is linked from PMLP’s application index. A previous candidate URL, `/en/media/100/download`, was not linked by the current index and was not relied upon. The current attachment is `/en/media/1666/download`.

This field map records the full substantive structure and the conditional notes shown in the current PDF. It is an aid to finding fields, not a replacement for the official form.

### I. Purpose of entry/residence

- Reason for residence.
- Requested duration: permanent residence or residence until a specified date.

### II. Applicant’s personal details

The form requests:

- Given name(s), surname, family/birth name, and former names where changed.
- Date of birth; former date if the birth date has been changed.
- Place of birth by country, province/state/region, and district/town/settlement.
- Sex, citizenship(s), and nationality/ethnicity. A stateless applicant is asked to state the country of residence.
- Foreign residence address and telephone number.
- Intended residence address in Latvia, with administrative area and locality fields, street, building/house, block, apartment, and telephone.

If residence is connected with family reunification and a birth certificate is needed to prove kinship, the form provides fields for the birth registration entry (number, date, country, office) and birth certificate (number, issue date, issuing country and authority).

Where the intended address is also the declared Latvian residence, the form asks for the legal basis: ownership or a right to use the property, with options for a written rental contract, lease, marriage, relationship, agreement with the owner or another basis. It asks for the relevant person/owner/lessor or other legal-basis details. An additional-address field records locality and the period from/to.

### III. Travel document

Fields cover travel-document type and number, issuing country and authority, issue and expiry dates, and any personal identity number shown in the travel document.

### IV. Permission to reside in another country

This section is for a family member of a Union citizen who is not a Union citizen. It asks whether the applicant has permission to reside in another country and asks for legal status in the country of residence. Listed choices include refugee status, permanent residence permit, temporary residence permit and visa.

### V. Marital status and spouse/partner

The form asks the applicant to select a marital status (married, single, divorced, or widowed). Where residence relates to family reunification and the information is needed to prove kinship, an applicant who is married or has a registered or unregistered partnership supplies details about the spouse/partner:

- Name, date of birth or personal identity number, place of birth and citizenship.
- Residence address.
- Date and country/place of marriage or partnership registration.
- Registry entry number and certificate number, date, issuing country and issuing authority/place.

### VI. Relatives

This section is completed when residence relates to family reunification and the information is needed to prove kinship. It provides tables for:

- The applicant’s and spouse’s children, including adult children.
- The applicant’s parents.
- Persons under the applicant’s guardianship, trusteeship or custody.

The tables request names, dates of birth, sex, citizenship, marital status and addresses. For guardianship/trusteeship, further fields record establishment date, country and authority, registry entry number and duration.

### VII. Additional information

The applicant may identify emergency contacts in the foreign country and Latvia (relatives or friends), with each person’s name, birth details, address and telephone number.

### VIII. Declarations and signature

The applicant is asked to attest to the form’s declarations. In summary, these cover:

1. Answers are complete and truthful.
2. The applicant lives or will live at the declared address.
3. PMLP will be notified in writing within three days if the circumstances underlying the residence document cease or change, or if the applicant moves abroad permanently.
4. The applicant acknowledges PMLP’s ability to clarify facts and check submitted information.
5. The applicant makes a declaration concerning specified infectious diseases identified in Cabinet rules.
6. The applicant declares possession of a document from a competent EU, EEA or Swiss authority establishing entitlement to planned healthcare services.
7. The applicant states the means of subsistence available and their amount; the form notes this requirement does not apply to workers and self-employed persons.
8. The applicant confirms understanding the questions and explanations in a language they understand or with an interpreter’s help.

The form includes date, signature and printed-name fields, plus an agency-use section for Latvian-name rendering, receiving official, date/signature, notes, case number and the residence document decision particulars.

## Source status and limits

The form PDF is a fillable administrative instrument. This Markdown field map does not reproduce its 182 interactive fields, layout, checkboxes or signature blocks and is not a substitute application form. Download and complete the current PDF linked by PMLP. Do not rely on the older direct PDF at `/en/media/100/download`; PMLP’s current page links the attachment at `/en/media/1666/download`, updated 23 October 2026.

The detailed declaration text is represented in paraphrase. It describes statements printed in the form; it does not establish that each declaration is a legal requirement in every case or that the form itself accurately states all present legal requirements. Check the new Latvian free-movement Act and effective implementing rules for EU/EEA/Swiss cases, and the relevant immigration rules for any other route. The source pack records unresolved transitional questions concerning Regulation 675 and applications after 10 July 2026.
