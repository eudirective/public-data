# State Border Guard of Latvia: official entry and border-information site

**Source ID:** lv-35  
**Publisher:** State Border Guard of Latvia  
**Official homepage:** https://www.rs.gov.lv/en  
**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026  
**Record type:** maintained authority and navigation record, not a complete entry rulebook or appeal procedure.

## Homepage scope

The English homepage identifies the State Border Guard and links to person border crossing, vehicle border crossing, borderland regime, visa requirements, and travel documents. It also publishes news and dated operational notices. The homepage displayed October 2026 notices on border activity and enforcement; those are operational examples, not complete legal guidance.

## Linked-page review

- [Person border crossing](https://www.rs.gov.lv/en/person-border-crossing) was updated 2 September 2025. It cites Cabinet Regulation No. 675 for EU citizens and family members. That citation predates the new free-movement Act effective 10 July 2026; do not rely on it as current without reconciliation.
- [Vehicle border crossing](https://www.rs.gov.lv/en/vehicle-border-crossing) was published 19 August 2020. It lists driving and vehicle documents and border insurance checks. No later update date is displayed.
- [Borderland regime](https://www.rs.gov.lv/en/borderland-regime) was published 19 August 2020. It describes a borderland up to two kilometres wide, special passes and exceptions. No later update date is displayed.
- [Visa requirements](https://www.rs.gov.lv/en/visa-requirements) was published 19 August 2020. It describes visa categories, consular competence and supporting documents, but includes an obsolete Schengen-country count and historical fees, including amounts in Latvian lats. Do not use it as a current fee or procedure guide.
- [Travel documents](https://www.rs.gov.lv/en/travel-documents) was updated 9 December 2024. It describes travel-document criteria but cites the former Immigration Law. The new Immigration Law took effect on 15 September 2026; verify current requirements independently.

The child pages are separate sources and may change independently of the homepage. This note confirms their identity, visible dates and notable limitations; it does not accept their detailed legal instructions as current.

## Coverage limit

The State Border Guard site is an official route for border-crossing and operational information. This review did not confirm a dedicated refusal-of-entry procedure, appeal deadline or remedy. Search official legal and remedy sources separately when those issues matter.

## Maintenance

At each review, compare the homepage service links and current notices, then check each relevant child page's date, legal citations, attachments and procedures separately. Keep refusal and appeal coverage open until an authoritative procedure or remedy source is identified. Do not treat the homepage or news items as proof of complete legal coverage.
