# PMLP: Family unification under the general immigration route (dated guidance)

**Source ID:** lv-34  
**Publisher:** Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP), Latvia  
**Official page:** https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/family-unification  
**Page metadata observed:** published 14 October 2020; updated 22 April 2022  
**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026  
**Record type:** maintained, dated source note; structured paraphrase of a PMLP page, not a current application checklist.

## What the page says

The page describes a residence-permit application route for family unification with a Latvian citizen, Latvian non-citizen, or foreigner holding a permanent residence permit. The described relative category is limited to relatives within the listed third-degree vertical or horizontal relationship, or a brother/sister-in-law within the stated degree.

For the applicant, the page lists:

- a full passport copy, which the foreigner certifies as valid;
- the specified residence-permit application form;
- a photograph;
- a criminal-record statement from the country of citizenship or residence for a foreigner over 14 who has lived in that country for more than 12 months;
- evidence of subsistence, stated on the page as EUR 500 per month for an adult and EUR 150 per month for a minor;
- proof of state-duty payment; and
- documents proving the family relationship.

It also says the inviter must have the invitation approved by PMLP before the application is submitted. For an accompanying minor or dependent child, it calls for the listed documents for that child (excluding the marriage certificate), proof of relationship, and permission from the other parent, notarised or signed in the presence of an administrative-body or representation officer.

This note condenses the page's wording. It does not reproduce its form or the linked application materials.

## Currentness and legal-use limit

The live page displays an update date of 22 April 2022. Latvia adopted a new Immigration Law on 20 August 2026, effective 15 September 2026. PMLP announced that the new law changes residence-permit requirements and application language rules. The 2022 page's relationship categories, application documents, invitation process, and EUR 500/EUR 150 subsistence figures have not been reconciled against the new law and current Cabinet rules.

Treat this note as evidence of what the official page stated on its displayed date. Do not use it as a current third-country family-unification checklist or rely on its listed financial thresholds without checking the in-force law and current PMLP instructions. This general immigration route is distinct from EU free-movement family rights.
