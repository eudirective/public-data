## Who the page describes

The PMLP checklist links to a category-specific list for “A family member of a Union citizen.” The linked page says a family member who is **not a Union citizen** may request a registration card or residence permit when entering Latvia together with the Union citizen. It lists the evidence below. The page should not be read as establishing every category of qualifying family member or replacing the governing Latvian and EU/EEA/Swiss legal rules.

The main “What documents must be filed?” page also provides separate checklist links for a Union citizen or family member applying on the basis of employment, self-employment, service provision, study, sufficient resources, marriage to certain Latvian residents, guardianship, and family relationship. Those are separate routes; this note records the linked non-EU family-member checklist.

## Documents listed by PMLP

The specific family-member page lists:

1. **Standard questionnaire.** The page links to a standard questionnaire, whose linked page displays an update date of 9 February 2026. Check the current PMLP page and download the live form before filing. The family-member page says this item applies provided the applicant has not previously obtained a registration card or residence permit.
2. **Travel document.** A copy of a valid travel document, including every page containing an entry or marking. The submitter confirms that the copy is correct and records the number of pages, name, date and signature.
3. **Relationship evidence.** A document confirming marriage, kinship or registered partnership.
4. **Sufficient resources.** A certification that the applicant has sufficient resources not to become a burden on Latvia’s social assistance system. PMLP says an appropriate entry in the questionnaire serves as the certification when that questionnaire is filed. Without it, the applicant may provide a free-form application stating the source and origin of the sufficient subsistence means.
5. **Healthcare or insurance.** A document issued by a Member State certifying entitlement to necessary medical or emergency medical assistance in Latvia, or a health-insurance policy.
6. **Consent for a minor.** If the residence document is sought for a minor, PMLP lists a letter of consent signed by both parents or a guardian and either notarised or signed before a PMLP or mission official, authorising the child to travel to Latvia. If the child will reside with one parent, the consent is required from the parent not entering Latvia with the child.
   - PMLP says consent is not required where the child enters Latvia with both parents or a guardian.
   - Where the entering parent exercises guardianship and there is no restriction on changing the child’s residence without the other parent’s consent, PMLP asks for the relevant court ruling or decision of another competent authority or official.
7. **Extended-family or dependency evidence.** A document confirming a joint household, dependency on the Union citizen, or (where serious health problems are relied on) the need for personal care and the Union citizen’s undertaking to provide that care.
8. **Unregistered partnership evidence.** PMLP lists a document confirming the birth of a child in an unregistered partnership, or documents showing that the unregistered partnership has lasted at least two years. Examples include a joint mortgage facility, real property or household evidence.

### Published exemption for Union-citizen workers

The page says family members do not need to provide the sufficient-resources certification or the healthcare/insurance document if the Union citizen is employed in Latvia or self-employed there. The page does not state this exemption for every other status; do not extend it to students, economically inactive citizens or other cases without checking current law and instructions.

## Filing and related procedure

The PMLP filing-information page, which says it was updated 24 July 2025, states that a Union citizen or family member who is in Latvia may file first-time registration/residence-document applications with any territorial PMLP division, electronically with a secure electronic signature or by post. It says a non-Union family member who does not reside in Latvia submits the documents to a Latvian embassy abroad. This filing page also predates the new residence law and is recorded here only as the content PMLP published on that page at review; verify the current route with PMLP or the relevant Latvian mission.

The related central PMLP overview was updated 17 July 2026. It says Latvian entry and residence for EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members is governed by Directive 2004/38/EC and Latvia’s law on entry and residence of these groups. It distinguishes registration cards and permanent-residence cards for Union citizens from residence permits and permanent residence permits for family members who are not Union citizens. The newer overview does not, by itself, confirm that every detail of the older linked checklist remains applicable under the new law.

## Source and legal limitations

- Latvia’s Law on Entry and Residence of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members took effect on 10 July 2026. PMLP’s detailed checklist page is dated 24 July 2025 and the specific family-member page is dated 19 June 2024. The currently linked questionnaire shows a later 9 February 2026 update, but that does not establish that all checklist requirements were revised for the new Act.
- The official Regulation 675 record displays a 2 December 2027 end-of-validity date, while the new Act’s transitional rules address applications filed before commencement and provide for future Cabinet procedures. The applicability of the old regulation to later applications remains unresolved in the source review. Do not use this checklist or an old Regulation 675 citation to resolve that legal question.
- The page is administrative guidance. It is not a complete statement of Directive 2004/38, the new Latvian Act, its implementing rules, or the scope of individual rights.
- For a particular filing, check the current Latvian text of the new Act, effective Cabinet rules and forms, PMLP’s live page, and the competent mission’s instructions. Distinguish EU, EEA, Swiss and UK Withdrawal Agreement routes.
