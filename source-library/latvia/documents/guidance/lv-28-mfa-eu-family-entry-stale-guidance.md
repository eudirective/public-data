## What the page says

The live English MFA page consists of a brief statement: entry into and residence in Latvia for citizens of the EU, EEA and Switzerland and their family members is provided for by Cabinet Regulation No. 675 of 30 August 2011, “Procedures for the Entry and Residence in the Republic of Latvia of Citizens of the Union and their Family Members.” It links to PMLP information on Union-citizen entry and a separate page about visa facilitation for qualifying family members.

The page displays a publication date of 5 July 2021 and an update date of 17 June 2026. The substantive text and linked destinations were opened and checked on 29 October 2026.

## Currentness and legal-use limit

This page's last displayed update predates the new Latvian Law on the Entry and Residence of Citizens of the European Union, the European Economic Area, and the Swiss Confederation and Their Family Members, which took effect on 10 July 2026. PMLP's central EU/EEA/Swiss entry page was updated on 17 July 2026 and now identifies that new law, while the MFA page continues to present Regulation No. 675 as the governing instrument.

Treat the MFA statement as evidence of the Ministry's published guidance and of a verified stale-guidance conflict. Do not rely on it to determine the current procedure or the post-commencement applicability of Regulation No. 675. The official Regulation record displays an end-of-validity date of 2 December 2027, while the new Act's transitional provisions leave the relationship for applications filed on or after 10 July 2026 unresolved. The MFA page does not resolve that issue.

The visa-facilitation link is a separate source and requires its own currentness and scope review.
