## What the page says

The page describes these routes and documents:

- A Union citizen may receive a registration card in listed situations: employment, self-employment, service provision (including employment by a provider established in another Member State), study with sufficient resources and health coverage, sufficient resources and health coverage, specified spouse relationships with sufficient resources and health coverage, or Latvian trusteeship/guardianship.
- For the sufficient-resources categories, the page defines the threshold as monthly income of at least 50% of the applicable minimum wage.
- It lists circumstances where a Union citizen may remain beyond three months without registering with PMLP: residence for up to six months while seeking work (with continued residence possible where the person continues to seek work and has a genuine chance of employment), a cross-border worker returning to another EU Member State at least weekly, and a student whose intended Latvian residence does not exceed one year.
- It describes permanent residence for Union citizens after five years of continuous lawful residence, and separately lists a former Latvian citizen/non-citizen application within 30 days of losing that status and minor-child cases.
- It lists early permanent-residence routes for workers or self-employed people who retire or take early retirement under stated work/residence conditions, stop work because of permanent incapacity, or work in another Member State after three years while retaining and regularly returning to a Latvian residence.
- It says a family member may apply for permanent residence after five years of lawful residence, with an exception where the Union citizen has already received permanent residence, and describes a minor-child route.
- It describes a residence permit for a family member who is not a Union citizen: up to five years where the Union citizen's foreseeable stay is shorter than five years, and ten years where permanent residence has been granted.

These are the page's descriptions. Conditions and terminology above are condensed; consult the page and governing law for the complete wording.

## Currentness and legal-use limit

This page was last updated on 24 July 2025. Latvia's new Law No. 369282 on entry and residence of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members took effect on 10 July 2026. PMLP's central EU/EEA/Swiss entry page was updated on 17 July 2026 and now points to that new law, while this document-types page predates it. Its thresholds, categories, exceptions, and document durations have not been reconciled item-by-item against the new Act and current procedures. Treat this record as evidence of what the official page stated on its displayed update date, not as confirmation that each instruction remains operative for applications filed after 10 July 2026.

The unresolved role of Cabinet Regulation 675 for post-commencement applications is documented in the Latvia acceptance and dependency registers. Do not use this page alone to determine current eligibility, application steps, or legal status.
