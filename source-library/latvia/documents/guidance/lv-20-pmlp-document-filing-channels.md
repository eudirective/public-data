# PMLP: Where EU-family residence documents may be filed

## Filing routes stated on the page

For a Union citizen or family member **who is in Latvia**, PMLP says applications for a first-time registration certificate, permanent-residence certificate, residence permit or permanent residence permit may be submitted to any territorial division of the Office:

- electronically, with each document confirmed by a secure electronic signature; or
- by registered or ordinary post, with an email address and telephone number for contact included in the application documents.

PMLP adds two location-specific limits for first-time applications:

- A Union citizen may request a first-time registration certificate only while in Latvia.
- A family member of a Union citizen who is not a Union citizen and does not reside in Latvia must submit the documents to a Latvian embassy abroad.

For a **duplicate** of a residence document, the page says a Union citizen or family member who is in Latvia or abroad may submit the request to any territorial PMLP division:

- electronically, with each document confirmed by a secure electronic signature; or
- by registered or ordinary post, providing an email address and telephone number in the application documents.

The page links to the list of [PMLP territorial divisions](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/territorial-divisions) for offices.

## Scope, dates and legal dependency

The published instructions distinguish first-time requests from duplicate-document requests, distinguish a Union citizen from a non-Union family member abroad, and describe electronic and postal submission. They do not state that in-person filing is available for the applications described. Do not infer unlisted exceptions or filing channels.

The related PMLP overview, [Entry of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/entry-citizens-eu-eea-and-switzerland-and-their-family-members), was updated 17 July 2026 and names Latvia’s EU/EEA/Swiss residence law. This filing-channel page is older (updated 24 July 2025). The later overview does not itself confirm that each older filing instruction was revised after the law changed.

Latvia’s new Law on Entry and Residence of EU, EEA and Swiss Citizens and their Family Members took effect on 10 July 2026. Its transitional provisions, future Cabinet procedures, and the displayed validity date of Cabinet Regulation 675 leave the relationship between some pre-existing operational instructions and post-commencement applications unresolved in the current source review. Verify the rules and live instructions for the particular application date and applicant category. The page is administrative guidance, not legislation.
