## Currentness and scope

The hub was updated on 20 May 2026, before the new Latvian free-movement Act took effect on 10 July 2026. In particular, its Union-citizen/family-member link must be checked against current law: the MFA child page still cites Cabinet Regulation No. 675, while the current PMLP overview (updated 17 July 2026) refers to the new law. That conflict is documented separately in the maintained [MFA EU-family entry note](lv-28-mfa-eu-family-entry-stale-guidance.md).

The page's other travel, border and visa links are not covered by that EU-family conflict finding. Each linked page has its own publisher date and legal scope and must be checked separately before relying on it. This note accepts the live MFA hub as an official navigation page, not as comprehensive current entry or visa guidance.
