## What the page says

The page groups guidance on personal identity numbers, residence declaration, and Latvian identity registration for a child born abroad:

- It says a foreigner is allocated a Latvian personal identity number upon receipt of a temporary or permanent residence permit, and that a visa alone does not confer the number.
- For a citizen of another EU Member State, it says a person planning to stay permanently in Latvia for more than 90 days should request a Union-citizen registration certificate. It links to PMLP document guidance.
- It mentions a foreigner's eID as an option available under certain criteria, describing it as an identity document for digital and in-person service use, including for people who do not permanently reside in Latvia.
- It says a foreigner staying in Latvia with a valid residence permit may declare an address through the Latvija.lv e-service, the local-government residence-declaration institution for the new address, or a PMLP territorial unit when receiving the residence permit.
- For a child of a Latvian citizen born abroad, it directs the parent to submit an application and documents to PMLP to register the child as a Latvian citizen. It says submission may be by post or by email with a secure electronic signature.

The page links out for the specific registration-document checklist, foreigner eID, address declaration and citizenship-registration process. Those linked procedures are separate sources and may have changed independently.

## Currentness and legal-use limit

The page displays a publication date of 31 January 2022 and no later update date. Its statement that an EU citizen planning to stay longer than 90 days should request a registration certificate is not accepted as the current registration threshold. Latvia's new Law No. 369282 on entry and residence of EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members took effect on 10 July 2026; the updated central PMLP entry page now refers to that law. Registration conditions and exceptions must be determined from the current Act and current PMLP procedures.

The residence-address passage is framed for a foreigner with a residence permit. It does not establish which declaration duties or channels apply to Union citizens in every situation. Likewise, the eID and child-citizenship sections are dated pointers, not confirmation of current eligibility or procedure. Use the live source and current linked services for those separate questions.
