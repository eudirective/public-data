## Scope and content

The Ministry presents this as orientative information and says the rules in force on the filing date control. It covers permanent residence cards for qualifying non-EU/EEA/Swiss family members of Spanish, EU, EEA or Swiss citizens. It describes four routes to permanent residence, family relationship and dependency categories, supporting evidence, the EX-19 form, filing through the immigration office or Police, and electronic filing through Mercurio. It also refers users to current law and procedural pages.

The page lists Directive 2004/38/EC, Real Decreto 240/2007 and Orden PRE/1490/2012. Its note distinguishes Spanish nationals who exercised free movement (EX-19 route) from other Spanish-national-family cases (EX-24 route). Operational grouping of EU, EEA and Swiss nationals does not erase the distinct legal bases.
