## Scope and content

Hoja 65 is an orientative Ministry sheet about the EU citizen registration certificate. The page and PDF both direct readers to the National Police procedure for current requirements, conditions and documents. The Ministry says the rules in force when an application is filed apply. The PDF adds no separate substantive checklist; it repeats the dated signposting function of the HTML.
