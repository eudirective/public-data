## Scope

This is operational guidance for appeals from residence-case decisions issued at first instance by a voivode. It is not a universal rule for every immigration decision. In particular, the page says appeals from voivode work-permit decisions go to the minister responsible for labour. Asylum, visa, employment and other special procedures may have different authorities or deadlines. Start with the exact decision's instructions and governing special law.

The MOS portal may provide a form/template to help prepare an appeal, but the appeal itself is not submitted through the online residence-application flow. The target page directs the appeal to the Head of the Office for Foreigners, through the voivode who issued the decision; send it to that voivode rather than directly to the Office's filing office. Direct submission to the appeal authority may cause forwarding and delay.

## Main steps stated in the official MOS guidance

- **Ordinary deadline:** 14 days from service of the first-instance decision.
- **Late appeal:** A request to restore the deadline may be made only if the party makes plausible that the failure was without fault. The request must be filed within 7 days after the cause for missing the deadline ends, and the missed appeal must be filed at the same time. The deadline for requesting restoration itself cannot be restored.
- **Where to file:** Address the appeal to the Head of the Office for Foreigners and submit it through the voivode who issued the contested residence decision.
- **Identification and contents:** Sign the appeal and make the appellant identifiable by name and full address. Identify the decision (issuing authority, date, reference number and subject) and state a clear request. A representative should include proof of authority if it is not already in the file; the page says the original or certified copy may be needed and notes stamp duty.
- **Decision-specific caveat:** The page distinguishes work-permit appeals, which it says go to the minister responsible for labour. Use the authority and deadline in the decision's appeal instruction where a special statute applies.

The MOS page presents these steps as practical guidance. The current Code of Administrative Procedure (KPA) provides the controlling general framework: Article 128 says an appeal generally need not contain detailed reasons if it shows dissatisfaction, subject to special statutory requirements; Article 129 provides filing through the authority that issued the decision and a 14-day period, subject to special-law periods; Articles 58–59 govern restoration of a missed time limit. For an individual case, confirm the correct appeal path, whether execution is suspended, and any special time limit from the decision and current legislation.
