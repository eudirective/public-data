## Confirmed source and scope

The official indexed result identifies the page as “Obywatel UE” published by UDSC. Its indexed text addresses EU citizens who want to work and remain in Poland. It states that EU citizens may work without a work permit and that a person intending to remain over three months should register residence personally with the competent voivode using the designated form. The index states an application deadline no later than the day after the first three months from entry. Treat these as the page's guidance summary, not a substitute for the governing Act or a case-specific deadline calculation.

The page is a central-authority overview. It does not supply a verified complete family-member procedure, current form set, voivode appointment/logistics, full evidentiary list, remedy route, or a current consolidated legal text. Related UDSC and MOS pages and the Act on entry into, residence in and departure from Poland by EU Member State citizens and their family members must be checked for those issues. Polish is the page language; the relevant Polish legal text controls.
