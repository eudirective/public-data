# Free legal aid and citizen advice for foreigners in Poland

- **Jurisdiction:** Poland
- **Source ID:** `pl-36`
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Justice, Republic of Poland (gov.pl)
- **Official source:** [Pomoc dla cudzoziemców — Free legal aid for foreigners](https://www.gov.pl/web/nieodplatna-pomoc/pomoc-dla-cudzoziemcow)
- **Language:** Polish
- **Checked:** 29 October 2026
- **Displayed revision date:** None exposed on the page.
- **Source-copy status:** Maintained summary of the official service page; the live directory is not copied.

## Verified service information

The page states that Poland's free legal assistance system is available to all persons staying in Poland, including foreigners. It says applicants do not first need to apply for temporary or international protection. Foreigners may use free legal aid or free citizen advice at a nearby in-person point or remotely by phone or internet.

The Ministry describes a network of specialist points for fields of law, including migration and refugee law. It also lists providers who speak English, Ukrainian or Russian and recommends that foreigners first seek a specialist point or provider from the displayed list.

The page includes a dynamic, filterable directory of points, with filters for voivodeship, county, point, weekday and language. The live page displayed 2,959 numbered entries when checked. Addresses, telephone numbers, times, providers, language availability and point availability can change and should be checked directly in the current directory.

Check the current law, the live directory, location, language, appointment and remote-access arrangements before relying on a listed provider.
