## Scope and source-grounded procedure

MAECI describes the registration in an Italian municipality of foreign judgments, including judgments concerning divorce, adoption, and changes of name or surname. It says Law No. 218/1995 provides, as a general rule, for automatic effectiveness in Italy of foreign judgments that meet basic compatibility requirements. The page separately directs readers to its international-adoption section for adoption judgments. This general page is procedural guidance; it does not determine whether a specific judgment is recognized, which law governs, or whether an EU instrument, treaty, or special procedure applies.

The page says the foreign judgment is to be registered with the competent Italian municipality. It describes two submission channels for a judgment issued abroad:

- Submit it directly to the Italian municipality; or
- Submit it to the Italian consulate for the district in which the judgment was issued.

The listed materials are:

1. A valid identity document.
2. An application requesting transmission of the judgment, in the form of a declaration in lieu of affidavit under Article 47 of Presidential Decree No. 445/2000. The declaration attests to the requirements referred to in Article 64 of Law No. 218/1995 and states that the judgment is not contrary to another judgment pronounced by an Italian judge and that no proceeding is pending before an Italian judge concerning the same subject matter and the same parties.
3. A complete copy of the judgment meeting the Article 64 requirements, duly legalised and translated into Italian.

These are the central page’s general instructions for the described route. The precise documents, authentication, translation and transmission process may depend on the type of judgment, issuing state, applicable EU instrument or convention, bilateral/multilateral agreement, and consular district. MAECI advises contacting the territorially competent diplomatic or consular representation in advance to check further details and any additional treaty-based requirements.

## EU divorce instruments: language-version discrepancy

The two official language pages are not aligned on the EU divorce rules:

- The **Italian page**, checked 29 October 2026, refers to Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1111. It identifies Annex II for judicial decisions and Annex VIII for separations or divorces concluded in an authentic instrument or registered agreement. It states that the certificates referred to in Article 90 are exempt from legalisation, and that the certificates are completed in the language of the decision, authentic instrument or agreement, or another official language.
- The **English page**, also opened on 29 October 2026, still refers to Council Regulation (EC) No 2201/2003 and its Article 22. The English page therefore contains an outdated instrument reference for current cases under the newer regime.

Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 states that it applies from 1 August 2022 and repealed Regulation (EC) No 2201/2003 from that date, subject to the transition provision in Article 100(2). See the [official EUR-Lex text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R1111), in particular Articles 100, 104 and 105. The applicable regime can depend on when the relevant proceedings were instituted or the instrument/agreement was drawn up or registered. Do not apply the old or new certificate route solely from this summary: check the governing regulation’s temporal provisions and the competent authority’s current instructions.

## Adoption and other limits

The MAECI page expressly refers adoption judgments to a dedicated section. Do not use this general transcription checklist as a complete adoption-recognition procedure. Adoption cases can engage separate Italian legislation, international conventions, EU rules, and the child’s circumstances.

The page’s general statement about automatic effectiveness does not remove the need to establish the applicable recognition conditions or complete the requested registration/annotation. The maintained source note does not reproduce the complete text of Article 64 or decide whether its requirements are satisfied in an individual case. Check the current official text of Law No. 218/1995, the applicable EU or treaty instrument, and the instructions of the responsible municipality or consulate.
