# France source list (reviewed 2026-10-30)

**Scope:** bounded discovery of official French sources related to EU/EEA/Swiss free movement, immigration, family and civil status, visa/residence practice, administrative procedure and remedies, courts, access to information, privacy, oversight, and legal aid. France is an EU Member State; EU, EEA EFTA, and Swiss legal routes remain distinct.

**Library rule:** Hyperlinks in this register are official-record locators or provenance pointers only. A URL is not an accepted source document and does not count toward the source count. Accepted text is retained locally under `documents/`; project-authored summaries are labeled as summaries and are not presented as official text.

**Inventory:** 26 accepted local documents: 19 retained official source texts (8 Service Public/DILA guidance or procedure records, 9 DILA LEGI statutory texts, 1 CNIL text, and 1 Conseil d’État source guide) and 7 project-authored maintained summaries. The register separately contains 32 official locator records and 1 N-Lex discovery-only record; locator and discovery URLs are not source documents. The 2018 DTD's applicability to current LEGI data remains open for human review. The 18 legacy candidate captures and retrieval/conversion history remain in the Monitor's local-only archive.
