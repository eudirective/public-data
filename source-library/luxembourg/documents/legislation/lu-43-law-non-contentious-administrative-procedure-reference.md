## Source identity and status

Legilux identifies the instrument as the Law of 1 December 1978 regulating non-contentious administrative procedure, published in Mémorial A No. 87/1978, page 2486, with commencement on 31 December 1978. The official Ombudsman page links the law and describes its purpose as protecting the rights of persons in administrative proceedings. The government’s disciplinary-investigation commissioner also lists the 1978 Law alongside its implementing Grand-Ducal Regulation of 8 June 1979.

The law is the enabling framework for the implementing regulation. Article 4 of the law, quoted by an official Ministry of Home Affairs circular, applies the implementing rules to individual administrative decisions unless a special text provides a procedure with at least equivalent safeguards. The regulation supplies much of the operational procedure. Treat the law and regulation as related but distinct sources.
