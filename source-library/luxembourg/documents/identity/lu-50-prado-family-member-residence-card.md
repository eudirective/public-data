## What this record identifies

PRADO classifies LUX-HO-11001 as an ordinary residence-related document issued by Luxembourg. Its stated purpose is to evidence the free-movement and residence right of a third-country national who is a family member of an EU citizen, or a citizen of Iceland, Norway or Liechtenstein. It describes a polycarbonate card, 86 × 54 mm, with a stated maximum validity of five years and no extension.

The Council's record is supplied by the General Secretariat and compiled from information provided by document experts in Luxembourg. It provides document-identification and examination information, not the legal eligibility conditions for a residence card.
