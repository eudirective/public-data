## What this source supports

The page gives the official administrative overview for EU citizens staying in Luxembourg beyond 90 days, with EEA and Swiss nationals treated as EU citizens for this purpose. It describes worker, self-employed, economically inactive, student and EU-family-member routes, the commune registration process, category-specific document lists, registration certificates and the five-year permanent-residence route. It also outlines recognition of durable unregistered partnerships and the administration's withdrawal procedure.

For registration beyond 90 days, the page says the arrival must be declared and registration completed at the commune within three months. Supporting evidence depends on the category. The separately linked financial-support statement is for a non-working EU/EEA/Swiss registration and is not itself residence authorization. The durable-relationship form is a preliminary recognition request; it is published in French. The power-of-attorney form is dated 2023 and should be checked for current use.
