# Croatia source discovery register

**Skill:** author-source-pack-rules v1.0.1
**Research date:** 24 October 2026
**Target:** Croatia, EU Member State. Compared with earlier free-movement and source-taxonomy findings from Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, and Romania; carried forward publisher, remedy, privacy, and sustainability issues from Austria, Belgium, and Bulgaria.
**Languages:** Croatian official sources; English official guidance where available.
**Scope:** legislation and guidance for free movement and third-country family members, visas, forms, general procedure, evidence, administrative remedies, privacy, oversight, legal aid, and courts. A located lead is not accepted and does not establish legal equivalence.

## Acquisition, archive and review outcome (source capture: 19 October 2026; link review: 24 October 2026)

The initial bounded HTTPS batch covered 41 seed records: 40 exact responses converted and replayed offline; `hr-e-predmet` returned an application/search surface without a stable text body. Twenty-five Narodne novine legal texts have selector-bound `.sl-content .doc` conversions with independent ordered-visible-text review, and four MUP EEA forms have page-by-page native-PDF review. Four new SIS/VIS forms were checked by complete text-layer extraction, page counts and PDF properties; their page images were not independently visually inspected. The resulting 34 accepted local documents are under documents/: 25 Croatian legal-text documents, eight MUP/MVEP forms and the MUP EEA guidance article body. The 25 source fragments, 51 raw source responses, 22 discovery/candidate notes and three acquisition manifests are in the EU Source Update Monitor’s indexed local-only archive. Their archive paths and hashes are recorded in source-acceptance.json; no candidates, captures or acquisition manifests remain in this country pack. The 35 official references in the acceptance register are usable by link within their recorded scopes; link acceptance remains separate from local-copy acceptance, topic coverage and country completion. Current MUP version indexes and legal-text cutoffs are recorded separately from the official links. Article 18(3) of Copyright Act NN 111/21 is applied to individually identified published official text and, narrowly, to the one unchanged MVEP VIS administrative form. MUP’s Terms of Use separately permit the seven unchanged MUP forms and the body of the identified EEA guidance page to be reproduced with source attribution. This does not cover page shells, third-party materials, other unreviewed page content or bulk/database-wide copies.

Search routes were cross-checked against the current MUP legislation index and N-Lex’s Croatian catalogue link.

## Bounded-search limitations

- The closest official government-published versions and listed updates are recorded; use those versions with their dates and known limits. Do not reconstruct the full amendment chain merely to create a consolidated text.
- Third-country 2026 regulation records, MVEP family-specific visa guidance, the full missions/VAC set, current privacy and court coverage were not all directly inspected. Use the closest government text for each source; no complete amendment-chain compilation is required.
- No applicant-specific forms, visa submissions, complaints, or case materials were collected.
- “No candidate identified” was not used as a conclusion that a source does not exist.
