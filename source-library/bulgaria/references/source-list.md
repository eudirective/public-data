# Bulgaria source discovery register

Research date: 25 October 2026; official-reference reviews: 21, 24, and 25 October 2026; original capture pass: 19 October 2026
Scope: official national sources relating to EU/EEA/Swiss family mobility, entry and residence, visas, administrative procedure, review, privacy, and oversight. Bulgaria is an EU Member State; each national route was checked independently. The comparison leads were the Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Hungary, and Romania source reviews.

This is a bounded register, not a complete inventory of Bulgarian law. It contains 72 source records, 17 accepted local documents, and 60 official references reviewed as usable by link within stated scope or cutoff. The 19 October capture run converted 24 of its original 35 leads and recorded 11 failures or blocks; those are historical retrieval outcomes, not current source-availability blockers. Source acceptance, local-copy status, topic coverage, and country completion are separate. This table is the current source register; individual link-use decisions and accepted local-copy paths are in source-acceptance.json, material cross-references are in source-dependencies.json, the canonical Bulgarian-language EU index routing is in shared-core.json, and discovery, disposition, retrieval, and capture working files are in the EU Source Update Monitor's local-only archive.
