## Main official routes

- The Ministry of Interior (MVR) Migration Directorate publishes the dedicated EU-citizen and family-member Act, its application forms, the Foreigners Act and implementing rules, and the Administrative Procedure Code. The MVR instruction on issuing EU-family residence documents is held locally through SG 29/2021. Keep the dedicated EU free-movement regime distinct from the general third-country-national regime.
- The Ministry of Regional Development and Public Works publishes the Civil Registration Act consolidation through SG 85/2024. It is held locally as supporting family-status law, with that cutoff recorded.
- The MVR and Ministry of Foreign Affairs publish consolidated Tariffs №4 and №3 respectively. Their PDFs state cutoffs and attribute the consolidated text to Ciela; those caveats are preserved.
- The MVR-published Bulgarian Personal Documents Act is a supporting source for residence-document issuance. The 19 November 2025 MVR-hosted Rules PDF's indexed Article 7(1)(10) gives a Migration filing route for residence-document applications under Personal Documents Act Article 59(2)-(4). The PDF predates Decree №286/2025, §2, published in SG 109/2025; that amendment is now recorded as a supplement. Direct retrieval of the PDF returned HTTP 403, and full-text currentness, later amendments, and card-specific instructions remain open. The official Administrative Register service entries 870 and 1403 add bounded filing, processing and appeal details, but their card titles still cite Regulation 2019/1157; they do not verify the current card format, and service 1403 displays both euro and lev fee amounts.
- The MFA normative-documents index links the Visa Ordinance consolidation through State Gazette issue 4/2025. The Commission for Protection against Discrimination hosts a Ciela consolidation of the Protection against Discrimination Act through issue 51/2026 (source date 19 June 2026). Decree №221/2025 §3 is the targeted later supplement. No Gazette-chain reconstruction is required.
- The canonical Bulgarian shared-core index now routes the Directive 2004/38 and Visa Code representations without duplicating shared files. Four cross-pack EU references are listed as external-only in `references/shared-core.json`: Regulation (EU) 2016/1191 on public documents, historical Regulation (EU) 2019/1157, successor Regulation (EU) 2025/1208, and the Visa Code digitalisation amendment, Regulation (EU) 2023/2667. The Bulgarian EU-citizen Act still refers to Regulation (EU) 2019/1157. The Court's effects-maintenance period ended when Regulation (EU) 2025/1208 entered into force on 10 July 2025. The successor's Bulgarian field-10 codes match the national wording, but the old legal citation and fee link remain, and no current card specimen or issuing instruction was located. Personal Documents Act § 24(3)'s 3 August 2026 date has passed; reconcile it with the successor's conditional transition to 2031 before making claims about current or individual cards. See `references/source-dependencies.json`; the national statutory wording is preserved.
- The CPDP Personal Data Protection Act, Ombudsman Act, and Ministry of Justice Legal Aid Act are accepted as link-only references within narrowly checked provisions and displayed cutoffs. Official NBPP forms, application guidance and administrative-service pages are retained as link-only routes; forms were not downloaded or accepted. The NBPP pages disagree on public hours, and electronic filing status conflicts with a 2019 notice, so these operational details remain unresolved. Direct page-opening attempts returned HTTP 502 or portal-shell responses for several official pages; their indexed text, identity, amendment date, and limitations are recorded in `references/source-acceptance.json`.
