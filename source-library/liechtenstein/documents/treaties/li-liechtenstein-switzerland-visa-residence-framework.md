## Reviewed subject matter

The agreement coordinates parts of the Liechtenstein and Swiss systems. It does not make the two states' immigration rules identical.

- **Articles 1–2 — purpose and implementing agreements:** Set the framework for cooperation on visa procedures, entry, residence, and border-area police cooperation in light of both parties' Schengen association. Further implementing agreements may supplement it.
- **Articles 3–5 — visa procedure:** Switzerland issues Schengen and Liechtenstein national visas on Liechtenstein's behalf. Liechtenstein authorities decide grant or refusal in consultation with Swiss authorities. The agreement allocates appeals differently for Schengen visas and Liechtenstein national visas and leaves procedural details to an implementing agreement.
- **Articles 6–12 — residence and related matters:** State the parties' free-movement arrangements for each other's nationals under the EFTA framework; address daily-return frontier workers, settlement after five years of qualifying residence, temporary residence and employment, cross-border services, removal and entry-ban cooperation, and representation in readmission and visa negotiations. Article 12 leaves details to a further agreement.
- **Articles 13–16 — border-area police cooperation:** Set out Liechtenstein's delegation of specified urgent police functions to the Swiss federal customs authority at the Liechtenstein–Austria internal border and within a defined border area, as well as joint controls and coordination if temporary internal-border checks are reintroduced. The operational arrangements are supplemented by agreement.
- **Articles 17–18 — data and coordination:** Permit necessary data exchange subject to domestic data-protection rules and establish a mixed commission to address interpretation and implementation.
- **Articles 19–22 — other instruments, term, and commencement:** List instruments repealed by the framework; preserve other treaty obligations, expressly including the EEA Agreement and the EU–Switzerland Agreement on Free Movement of Persons; provide for indefinite duration and termination; and state the entry-into-force rule.

## Application and dependencies

For visa matters, use this instrument with the current Liechtenstein entry and visa regulation (VEV, LR 152.204), the applicable Schengen acquis, and the relevant implementing agreements. The framework is not a mission-specific checklist and does not identify current appointment channels or prove that every Swiss representation accepts every application type under the same procedure.

For residence, keep the Liechtenstein–Switzerland arrangements distinct from Liechtenstein's EEA-specific free-movement solution. The agreement itself points to EFTA rules and preserves the EEA Agreement and the EU–Switzerland free-movement agreement. Its police-cooperation provisions concern specified border functions and should not be read as a general transfer of Liechtenstein police powers.

Related instruments identified by the official legal register include the visa-and-entry implementing agreement (LGBl 2011.567), residence implementing agreement (LGBl 2011.568), and border-area police cooperation agreement (LGBl 2009.218). They require separate source and status review.
