# Liechtenstein Migration and Passport Office family-reunification online form

**Source ID:** li-27  
**Publisher:** Migration and Passport Office (Ausländer- und Passamt, APA), Liechtenstein  
**Official form:** https://formulare.llv.li/formserver_APA/start.do?generalid=APA_FN  
**Reviewed:** 29 October 2026  
**Version evidence:** The live interactive form identifies itself as APA_FN and displays no publication or revision date. The public initial step was inspected; the form shows a five-step progress indicator.

## Verified first step

The form is titled **Familiennachzug** (family reunification). Its introductory text says that a separate application is required for each family member seeking a permit in Liechtenstein.

On step 1, **Art der Bewilligung** (type of permit), the required field **Nachzug von** (family reunification in relation to) offers:

- spouse or registered partner;
- child;
- parents;
- life partner; and
- other eligible persons.

The page identifies the form provider as E-Government and the recipient as the Migration and Passport Office at Giessenstrasse 3, 9490 Vaduz. It provides Deutsch and Englisch language options, but only the German view was verified. The visible form controls include navigation, saving entered data for later completion, and ending the form. No application data was entered or submitted.

## Scope and limits

This is a dynamic, multi-step application form, not a static document. Only its public initial step was verified in this review. The fields and requirements on steps 2–5, eligibility criteria, supporting-document requirements, validation behavior, and English-language rendering remain unverified. The general parent forms index was not resolved.

Use the official form for the complete, current workflow. This Markdown field map is a maintained locator and summary of the verified first step; it is not a substitute for completing or submitting the online form and should not be treated as a complete application checklist.
