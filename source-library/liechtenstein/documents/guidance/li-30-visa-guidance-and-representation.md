## What this source supports

The APA page states that Liechtenstein and Switzerland generally have the same entry rules and that their visa-required-person rules are largely aligned, subject to differences under bilateral or multilateral agreements. It directs users to the Swiss State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) or the Liechtenstein VEV to check whether a visa is required.

Visa-required applicants may generally submit an application at the Swiss representation where they reside abroad. Responsibility for deciding applications is divided between Swiss authorities and the Liechtenstein APA. The page lists locations where another Schengen state's representation is responsible instead, including specified posts of Austria, Hungary and Lithuania. The USA exception is limited to applications lodged in Chicago and the listed US states.

## Scope and limits

Use this page for Liechtenstein's official routing and authority overview. It does not establish a nationality's visa requirement by itself; use the current SEM check and VEV. Representation arrangements are location-specific and can change, so verify the competent post and its current intake process before applying. This page is about entry visas; residence permits, family reunification and EEA free-movement routes are separate procedures.

The page is published in German and exposes no clear revision date in the reviewed view. The summary is limited to information visible on 29 October 2026.
