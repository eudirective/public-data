# Liechtenstein source-rule sustainability assessment

Assessment date: 19 October 2026; source acceptance update: 25 October 2026. Scope: the 40 source leads in the inventory. This reviews retrieval and conversion behavior, not legal advice or coverage completeness. The PFZG, PFZV, AuG, ZAV, and VEV texts are accepted as five local sources with the limits recorded in [`source-acceptance.json`](source-acceptance.json); the rest of the pack remains under review.

## Implemented route and observed results

[`../sync_liechtenstein.py`](../sync_liechtenstein.py) performs ordinary allowlisted HTTPS GET requests with a 20-second timeout, a 24 MiB response cap, and a one-second delay. For Gesetze.li law records it first retrieves the stable LR/LGBl page, discovers the current-text iframe, then captures that exact frame and its version query. It preserves exact raw response hashes and produces Markdown from the legal body, official page content, or page-wise PDF text. PDF extraction uses pinned `pypdf==6.21.1`; `convert-pdfs` rebuilds it from preserved bytes and `verify` replays conversions offline. A 19 October refresh saved and replay-verified 16 HTML and two PDF conversions; the two original PDFs remain byte-preserved; 16 requests received HTTP 403; four staging routes were skipped; one interactive form and one case-law application shell did not yield complete source texts. On 25 October, PFZG and PFZV were accepted as local documents after version, identity, text, reuse and legal-classification review. PFZV version 15 displays a consolidation date of 1 January 2024 and the latest chronology entries are LGBl 2023.458 and .464, both effective that date. Its raw shell and text-frame responses were recovered from the standard temp area, hash-checked against the capture ledger, and archived through the Monitor. The legal-text body replayed exactly; the full-candidate check reports only a metadata preamble mismatch in publisher-version-evidence wording. Official HTML and 27-page PDF anchors were also checked. AuG version 25 was added after the current PFZG/PFZV cycle; its full conversion replay matches the saved candidate and the official 100-page PDF was reviewed at scope, family, removal, remedy, and 2026 amendment anchors. ZAV version 17 was then accepted as the AuG implementing regulation: its 2026.193 current version was confirmed from the official chronology, both raw HTML captures and the candidate replayed exactly, and selected anchors in the official 28-page PDF and version chronology were checked; there was no page-by-page comparison. VEV version 2 was accepted as a separate entry-and-visa regulation: its PDF states Fassung 12 October 2025; LGBl 2025.483 (effective that date) is the latest VEV amendment listed in the official 2026 Systematisches Register checked to 1 October. The candidate, raw shell/frame and retrieval manifest are preserved in the Monitor archive; full offline replay and selected anchors in the official 15-page PDF and amendment footnotes pass, with no page-by-page comparison. Its Schengen, VISV/EESV, bilateral and operational dependencies remain separate. Neither ZAV nor VEV acceptance establishes topic coverage. In the current runtime, the verifier confirms all HTML-backed entries and accepted documents but cannot replay the two legacy PDF records li-05 and li-13 because pinned pypdf==6.21.1 is unavailable. Other source records remain open.

The change detector retains exact source hashes and ignores only observed transport variation: scripts, Drupal view IDs, CSRF and SiteImprove tokens, a Liechtenstein CSS cache timestamp, and the rotating Cloudflare email-protection fragment. Meaningful HTML changes are staged for review rather than accepted automatically. The observed email fragment change affected only a generated `href`; the same contact link retained its stable `mailto` path.

## Verdict

**Fragile for a bounded review-candidate capture; not sustainable yet for unattended source maintenance.** Confidence: medium for the observed retrieval behavior, low for ongoing reuse authorization and full source coverage. The accessible route is repeatable in this environment and offline replay is deterministic, but legal authenticity, recurring-use terms, blocked-route coverage, and several conversion forms remain unresolved.

## Sustainability risks

1. **Legal-text version:** Use the latest Gesetze.li government-published consolidation as the working text and retain its informational/no-legal-effect notice and displayed edition. Consult signed electronic LGBl PDF/A for a specific conflict, commencement question, or when authentic promulgation itself matters; routine review does not require signature validation or reconstruction of the amendment chain.
2. **Reuse authorization:** The PFZG text-only copy has a source-specific basis in URG Art. 5(1)(a) and (2). Other source classes and the conditions for systematic database copying, storage, transformation, or scheduled refresh remain open.
3. **Publisher blocks:** The LLV host returned HTTP 403 across multiple candidate routes. The failed requests are recorded; the rule does not emulate a browser challenge or use an unapproved route. Obtain a publisher-supported route or keep those sources manual.
4. **Text fidelity:** The current HTML and PDF extractors are deterministic but not legally validated against representative complex statutes. PDFs retain page boundaries, but tables, forms, links, annotations, layout, and attachments need separate review.
5. **Partial indexes:** The case-law portal is selected and exposes no individual decision through the captured shell. An empty search cannot be treated as proof that no relevant judgment exists.
6. **Jurisdiction boundaries:** PFZG, Liechtenstein's EEA-specific solution, the Swiss treaty route, third-country residence rules, and the EWR incorporation chain must remain distinct source identities.

## Optional work for recurring local monitoring

Ordinary reference use does not depend on this capture pipeline. Use the latest government-published consolidation as the working text, label it informational, and check a particular signed Gazette item only when a material date, amendment, correction, or publication question affects the issue. Do not reconstruct the full Gazette history.

If recurring monitoring or local text retention is later needed, then check the applicable reuse terms, obtain a publisher-supported route for blocked pages, and validate representative conversions and relevant attachments. PDF signature validation and repeated change-cycle comparisons belong to that automated or retained-copy workflow, not to every legal-source review.

PFZG, PFZV, AuG, and ZAV are accepted with their recorded limits. Other outputs remain review candidates or unresolved leads. The rule must not be presented as a complete or legally authoritative Liechtenstein source pack.
